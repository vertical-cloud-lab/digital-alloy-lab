We want to measure, for new aluminium alloys (and some copper alloys) printed by laser powder bed fusion (LPBF), the quantities that the Thermo-Calc Additive Manufacturing Module and similar melt-pool models predict, so we can calibrate and validate them. Our printing is small-scale: single tracks, single-layer pads, and small cubes/coupons (desktop LPBF with a 200-500 W, 1070 nm fiber laser, ~50-100 um spot; plus an Aconity MIDI and a Concept Laser M2 elsewhere). Alloys: AlSi10Mg, Al-Mg-Sc-Zr (Scalmalloy-type), lean Al-Mg-Zr, Al-Mn, Al-Ce and Al-Cu alloys, and Cu alloys (Ni-Al bronze, Cu-Ni). Please review the peer-reviewed literature and, for each item below, give the measurement method(s), typical equipment, sample preparation, how many tracks/samples are needed, the analysis (with equations), the open-source code available, typical uncertainties, and published examples with DOIs (prefer Al and Cu alloys):

1. Melt pool width, depth and length from single-track cross-sections and top-view imaging: etchants for Al and Cu alloys, number of sections per track, conduction vs transition vs keyhole mode classification (depth/width ratio, normalized enthalpy deltaH/h_s from King et al., keyhole number / Ke from Gan et al.), and heat-source calibration (absorptivity, beam radius, Goldak/Gaussian parameters) against measured melt pools, including how the Thermo-Calc AM Module, Eagar-Tsai / Rosenthal models and FLOW-3D type CFD have been calibrated.
2. Laser absorptivity of Al and Cu powder beds and bulk at ~1070 nm: integrating-sphere and calorimetric methods (e.g., Trapp et al. LLNL, NIST integrating-sphere work), values vs power/speed/mode.
3. Thermal history, peak temperature, thermal gradient G and solidification rate R: thermocouples, two-colour pyrometry, IR thermography, and post-mortem proxies (cell/dendrite arm spacing vs cooling rate relations for Al-Si and Al-Mg alloys, R = v cos(theta) from melt-pool shape), and in-situ synchrotron X-ray imaging/diffraction (e.g., Advanced Photon Source 32-ID) measurements of melt pool, keyhole and solidification.
4. Columnar-to-equiaxed transition, grain size and texture in LPBF Al alloys (Sc/Zr inoculation): EBSD on tracks and pads, comparison with G-R maps and Hunt/KGT-type CET models.
5. Composition change by selective evaporation (Mg, Zn, Li loss in Al alloys; Zn in brasses): ICP-OES/EDS/EPMA of powder vs printed material, measured Mg loss vs energy density, comparison with evaporation models.
6. Printability maps: lack of fusion, keyholing, balling and hot cracking criteria; density (Archimedes, CT, optical image analysis) vs process parameters; keyhole porosity thresholds; crack density measurement; how to build printability maps from single tracks and pads with minimal powder.
7. Residual stress in small LPBF coupons: XRD sin^2(psi), bridge-curvature / cantilever deflection, contour method, hole drilling, neutron diffraction.
8. Spatter and vapour plume: high-speed imaging and schlieren, and their relevance for Al.
9. Powder properties that control LPBF of these alloys: particle size distribution (laser diffraction vs image analysis, ISO 13322 / ISO 13320), flowability (Hall/Carney, revolution powder analysis, FT4, avalanche/GranuDrum), apparent/tap density, oxygen and hydrogen content (inert gas fusion, ASTM E1019 / E1447), moisture.

End with a ranked list of the minimum set of single-track, pad and coupon measurements that best calibrates the AM module (or open alternatives) for a new Al alloy with only ~100-300 g of powder, and give a full reference list with DOIs.

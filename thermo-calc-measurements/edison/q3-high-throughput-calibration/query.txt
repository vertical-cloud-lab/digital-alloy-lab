We run a self-driving-lab style alloy discovery campaign for laser-powder-bed-fusion aluminium alloys (about 36 atomized compositions over 3 months, each a few hundred grams of powder, printed as single tracks, pads and small coupons). We want to know which characterization measurements of CALPHAD-predicted quantities can be automated, run in high throughput or on composition-graded / combinatorial samples, and fed back to calibrate thermodynamic, kinetic and property models. Please review the peer-reviewed literature and give quantitative throughput numbers (samples per hour, hands-on minutes per sample), equipment, and DOIs for:

1. Combinatorial and composition-graded metal samples for alloy screening: diffusion couples/multiples (e.g., J.-C. Zhao), thin-film libraries, laser- or DED-printed compositionally graded samples, in-situ alloyed LPBF from elemental powder blends, and arc-melted button arrays; what each is valid for (phase equilibria, diffusion, hardness, modulus, conductivity, corrosion).
2. Automated/high-throughput measurements: robotic or autosampler DSC/DTA, automated XRD mapping, automated SEM/EDS/EBSD, automated nanoindentation and hardness mapping, eddy-current or four-point-probe conductivity mapping, scanning droplet cell / automated potentiodynamic polarization for corrosion, high-throughput small-scale tensile testing, profilometry-based indentation plastometry, and automated Archimedes density; which have been integrated into closed-loop / autonomous experimentation for metals.
3. Feeding measurements back into CALPHAD and kinetic models: ESPEI / pycalphad dataset formats and Bayesian (MCMC) parameter fitting with new phase-equilibrium, DSC and thermochemical data; uncertainty quantification of CALPHAD predictions; calibration of precipitation (KWN / TC-PRISMA / kawin) model parameters such as interfacial energy from hardness, SAXS or DSC data; calibration of melt-pool/heat-source models from single tracks; and Bayesian/multi-fidelity approaches that combine CALPHAD with experiments in alloy design loops.
4. Reported discrepancies between CALPHAD (Thermo-Calc TCAL, pycalphad with open databases such as COST 507) and measurements for Al-Mg-Sc-Zr, Al-Si-Mg, Al-Mn, Al-Ce, Al-Cu and Al-Fe-Ni systems (solidus/liquidus, solubility of Sc/Zr/Mn, phase fractions), and for Cu-Al-Ni-Fe (nickel aluminium bronze) systems.

End with a ranked list of the 10 measurements with the best information-per-cost for such a campaign, flagging which can start within a month with common university equipment, and a full reference list with DOIs.

**Why it matters to the journal's readers.** New printable aluminum alloys are scarce because powder for an untested
composition is hard to get and because campaigns spend their budget in a fixed order. We address both: small-lot
ultrasonic atomization becomes a dependable step ([repeatability], [composition fidelity]), and the optimizer decides
for each candidate whether the next result is worth its cost. The closest published work in this journal designed an
aluminum alloy by composition-level optimization and then atomized, printed and tested it [Taheri-Mousavi et al.,
*Adv. Mater.* 2026, DOI 10.1002/adma.202509507]. Here the optimizer also chooses which step to run, and a digital
twin calibrated on our own campaigns shows when that beats screening and when it does not.

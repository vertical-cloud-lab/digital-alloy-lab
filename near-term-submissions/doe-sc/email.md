# DOE SC pre-application (white paper): how to submit

Submission is through DOE SC PAMS, not by email. The NOFO treats "white
paper" and "pre-application" as the same document: "A pre-application (also
called white paper) is recommended but optional for many topics" (Part 1,
Section IV.A.2, p. 105). Pre-applications "must be submitted electronically
through the DOE SC PAMS website ... Do not submit a pre-application through
FedConnect or Grants.gov" (Part 1, p. 107). Only the Experimental Condensed
Matter Physics topic asks for white papers "by email" (Part 1, p. 27); the
Synthesis and Processing Science topic does not.

Target date for Synthesis and Processing Science: November 30, 2026
(Part 1, p. 25). It is a target, not a hard deadline (Part 1, pp. 21-22).

## PAMS steps (Part 2, Section 6, pp. 17-18)

1. Register for a PAMS account at <https://pamspublic.science.energy.gov>
   (the PI must be registered; Part 2 asks for this at least a week before
   the target date).
2. Save the pre-application as one PDF with a file name of at most 50
   characters, e.g. `Baird-DOE-SC-preapp.pdf` (Part 2, p. 17).
3. In PAMS: Proposals tab > "View / Respond to Funding Opportunity
   Announcements" > find DE-FOA-0003665 > "Actions/Views" > "Submit
   Preproposal".
4. Institution: Brigham Young University. "Select PI": Sterling Baird.
5. Enter the title: *Nucleation, Freezing Range, and Hot Cracking in
   Aluminum Alloys Solidified at Laser Melting Rates*.
6. Program Manager dropdown: **Shawn Chen** (Synthesis and Processing
   Science; Part 1, p. 25; Part 1, p. 106 says to select the subprogram
   contact).
7. "Attach File": upload `white-paper.pdf` (renamed as in step 2).
8. Attach the list of senior/key personnel and the list of individuals who
   should not serve as merit reviewers as an "Additional Attachment"
   (Part 1, p. 106; content rules in Part 2, Section 10, pp. 25-26). This
   list is not written yet; see README.md.
9. Click "Submit to DOE". PAMS sends an acknowledgment from
   PAMS.Autoreply@science.doe.gov. The PI may submit directly; the NOFO
   allows the PI or anyone at BYU with the "Submit to DOE" privilege
   (Part 1, p. 107).

Warning from Part 2 (p. 18): revising a submitted pre-application in PAMS
removes the earlier version from consideration until the revision is
submitted.

PAMS help: 855-818-1846, PAMS-helpdesk@doe.gov (Part 1, Section I.C).

## Optional follow-up email to the program manager (send after step 9)

Part 1 encourages applicants to discuss ideas with the subprogram contact
(p. 21) and to "use your submitted pre-application or white paper to
initiate a discussion with the listed program manager" (p. 106).

**To:** shawn.chen@doe.gov
**Subject:** Pre-application in PAMS: rapid solidification of aluminum alloys (DE-FOA-0003665)

Dear Dr. Chen,

We submitted a pre-application in PAMS today to Synthesis and Processing
Science, titled "Nucleation, Freezing Range, and Hot Cracking in Aluminum
Alloys Solidified at Laser Melting Rates."

It asks why zirconium and scandium additions give equiaxed grains under some
laser conditions and columnar grains under others. It also asks why hot
cracking follows the end of the solidification curve, and how far CALPHAD
predictions depart from what forms at these rates. We make powder for each composition in our
lab and solidify single tracks over a grid of laser power and scan speed.

Does this fit the subprogram? We would also value your view on whether the
laser's local thermal field counts toward the emphasis on localized external
fields. We are glad to talk by phone at a time that suits you.

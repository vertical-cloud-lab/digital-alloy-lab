Dear AMDO Program Director,

As NSF 26-515 encourages, we attach a one-page summary of a proposal we plan to submit to the Advanced Manufacturing, Design, and Operations program.

The research asks how to decide, as data arrive, which step of a multi-step manufacturing workflow to run next and on which candidate. Our steps run from thermodynamic calculations through powder atomization, laser powder bed fusion, and microscopy to tensile tests, and they differ greatly in cost and time. We will extend cost-aware decision methods to several competing properties and to experiments whose results arrive at different times, and demonstrate them on printable high-strength aluminum alloys.

Does this topic fall within the scope of AMDO, or would it fit better in another program under NSF 26-515? We would also welcome a short call.

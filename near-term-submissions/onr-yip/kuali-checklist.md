# BYU routing (Kuali Research): what must be in by Fri Oct 23, 2026

ONR deadline: **Fri Oct 30, 2026, 5:00 PM ET** (3:00 PM in Provo), received by
Grants.gov (NOFO p. 7). BYU's Sponsored Projects Office (SPO) timeline,
counted back from Oct 30:

| Date | BYU step | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Fri Oct 16** (two weeks before) | Start the Kuali record and tell SPO about anything special (here: one equipment item; no cost share, no subawards). | SPO "Getting Started" [S1] |
| **Fri Oct 23** (five business days before) | Proposal complete in Kuali and **submitted for internal review**. | [S1] |
| **Wed Oct 28** (two business days before) | SPO submits to Grants.gov, system to system from Kuali. Matches the NOFO's advice (p. 28). | [S1] |

There is no separate "final technical documents" date: once a proposal is
routing, attachments can still be replaced, so the narrative can be polished
until SPO submits on Oct 28 [S3]. This is a single-PI proposal with no
subawards or cost share, so BYU's six-week "complex proposal" timeline does
not apply [S1].

**Approval chain in Kuali:** PI submits → research administration review →
department chair → associate dean → Associate Academic Vice President for
Research → SPO submits [S2, S4]. Approvers see the summary, attachments,
budget, personnel, questionnaire, compliance, and supplemental information,
and the chair is asked whether space and facilities are available [S2].

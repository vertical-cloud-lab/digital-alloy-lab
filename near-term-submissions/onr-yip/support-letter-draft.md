# Draft letter of support (BYU official)

The NOFO requires at least one letter of support, authored by the appropriate
university official(s); applications without one are incomplete and will not
be considered (NOFO p. 11). There is no required format. The letter must
articulate the university's long-term commitment to the applicant and his
research, and make clear that the applicant is viewed as a potential leading
faculty member and is truly outstanding (NOFO p. 19). It is review criterion
(4) (NOFO p. 30). Commitments the NOFO names as examples (p. 19): release time
from teaching or administrative duties, paid nine-month salary, other related
research funding, laboratory and computer investments, the start-up package,
support for graduate students and postdocs, and mentoring. The letter may also
describe the applicant's achievements. It is excluded from the page limit
(NOFO p. 16) and is attached to the proposal.

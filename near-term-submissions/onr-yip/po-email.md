```text
Dr. Fisher,

I plan to submit an FY27 YIP proposal (N0001426SF004) by October 30 and would like to check its fit with Materials & Processes for Additive Manufacturing.

Qualifying a new printed alloy runs through steps whose results take from minutes (thermodynamic calculations) to weeks (corrosion exposures), and most campaigns send every candidate through every step. I would build a method that decides, as results arrive, which measurement to take next and on which candidate. It extends cost-aware Bayesian optimization of partial results to competing properties and to experiments that finish at different times. The test case is printable high-strength aluminum for Navy sustainment. Corrosion resistance is one objective, and a one-hour polarization screen and longer salt-spray tests are steps the method chooses among.

The plan is a single-PI effort at about $250K per year for three years. Does this fit your program, and is Manufacturing (Integrated Manufacturing and Sustainment Technologies) the right focus area?

Thank you,
Sterling Baird
Assistant Professor, Mechanical Engineering, Brigham Young University
sterling.baird@byu.edu | (801) 422-5344
```

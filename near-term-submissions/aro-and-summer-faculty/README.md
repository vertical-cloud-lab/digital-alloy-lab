### ECP rules (Amendment 4)

| Rule | What the BAA says | Page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Timing | "Continuously open" through 20 November 2027; "Proposals may be submitted at any time while this BAA is open." | 2, 3 |
| Eligibility | U.S. citizens, U.S. nationals, or lawful permanent residents who have held a tenure-track position at a U.S. institution for fewer than five years at the time of application. If the institution does not use "tenure track," the proposal must say so and the supporting letter must state the PI will be considered for a permanent appointment. | 8 |
| Change from earlier notes | The Nov 2022 BAA said "Ph.D. or equivalent degrees within the last five years" (p. 8 of that version). Amendment 4 replaced this with the tenure-track rule above. | 8 |
| Amount | Up to $120,000 per year for 3 years. | 8 |
| Letter | Supporting letter from the department chair, dean, supervisor, or other official, showing long-term commitment (salary, release time, equipment, student support, cost share, start-up). Award goes to the institution. | 8 to 9 |
| Talk first | Discussion with the topic's ARO TPOC "is strongly recommended" before a white paper or proposal. | 8, 20 |
| Review criteria | Scientific merit; contribution to the Army mission; PI qualifications; long-term institutional commitment to the PI and the research. | 37 |
| Other agencies | The proposal must name other agencies receiving it. Concurrent submission "will not prejudice its review by ARL if ARL is kept informed." | 25 |

Related awards in the same BAA: STIR, $60,000 or less, 9 months, 10-page project description, no capital equipment, and no reliance on facilities outside the applicant's direct control (p. 7 to 8). Research Instrumentation, for U.S. institutions, budget to be discussed with the TPOC first (p. 10 to 11). PECASE is by invitation only (p. 9). I did not find the phrase "formerly ARO YIP" in the BAA, so that label is unverified.

### Steps after the call (Amendment 4, p. 20 to 22)

1. Send the white paper by email to the TPOC as one PDF of up to 5 pages. Include the title, PI and organization, topic name, topic ID, and TPOC name. Cover objective, approach, what is new, a rough cost estimate, and a short bio.
2. The TPOC replies "encouraged to submit a proposal" or "not encouraged to submit a proposal."
3. If encouraged, BYU submits the full proposal through Grants.gov Workspace, which the BAA requires for grants and cooperative agreements (p. 20). The proposal "is submitted by the institution" (p. 8), so route it through BYU's Research Administration Office.
4. Attach the supporting letter from the chair or dean, and name AFOSR as another agency receiving related work.

## Navy ONR Summer Faculty Research Program (SFRP)

The program has moved from navy.asee.org (which no longer resolves) to https://onroutreach-summer-faculty-research-sabbatical.com/ .

- Dates: https://onroutreach-summer-faculty-research-sabbatical.com/important-dates/
- Stipend, relocation, 10-week term, two-lab limit, background check: https://onroutreach-summer-faculty-research-sabbatical.com/summer-faculty-research-program/
- Steps: pick up to 2 labs, contact them, find a lab mentor ("not required, but extremely helpful"), write a research statement that matches the lab's work, create an account, upload CV details from the last 2 to 3 years, submit, then tell the mentor: https://onroutreach-summer-faculty-research-sabbatical.com/summer-faculty-research-program-application-process/

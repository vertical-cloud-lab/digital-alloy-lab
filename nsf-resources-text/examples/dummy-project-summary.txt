PROJECT SUMMARY

Project Title: AI-Guided Discovery of Critical-Mineral-Lean Aluminum Alloys
PI: J. Doe (Made-Up University)

(Page 1)

This proposal will develop a closed-loop machine learning platform for the
discovery of aluminum alloys that minimize use of critical minerals (Mn,
Zr, Cr) while maintaining structural performance. Over three years we
will (a) curate a CALPHAD-derived training set of 50,000 candidate
compositions, (b) implement a multi-fidelity Bayesian optimization
acquisition function that jointly considers manufacturability and
mechanical-property targets, (c) print and characterise approximately 200
candidate compositions on a laser-powder-bed-fusion system, (d) close the
loop by retraining the surrogate after every batch of 8 specimens, and
(e) release all data, models, and pipeline code openly.

The project builds on the PI's prior work on graph-neural-network
surrogates for high-throughput thermodynamics
(https://example.edu/doe-prior-work) and on the lab's existing partnership
with two domestic powder atomisation companies. Year 1 will focus on
data curation and surrogate training; Year 2 on the closed-loop pilot
campaign; Year 3 on scale-up to a second alloy family and on the public
release of the trained models and benchmark suite.

Expected outcomes include: a >10x reduction in time-to-discover for a
new alloy meeting a given property target relative to a Latin-hypercube
baseline; an open dataset of >1,000 LPBF tensile coupons with paired
process and property measurements; and a trained surrogate model that
the broader AI-for-materials community can fine-tune for related
problems. We will also train two graduate students and four
undergraduates in the intersection of ML and materials science, and
will run a one-week summer workshop for high-school teachers on
AI-for-materials curricula.

In summary, the proposed work integrates Bayesian optimization,
high-throughput characterisation, and CALPHAD modelling to address the
national strategic priority of reducing dependence on imported critical
minerals while advancing the state of the art in autonomous materials
discovery. Section 2 of this proposal describes the technical approach in
detail; Section 3 describes the team and facilities; Section 4 presents
preliminary results from a six-month feasibility study.

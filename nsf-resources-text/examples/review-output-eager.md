## Summary judgment

**Send to the cognizant Program Officer with two targeted reframes
(Findings 1 and 2) and one optional polish (Finding 3).** The 2-page
draft satisfies every required element of a PAPPG §I.D.1 concept
outline (title, proposal type, PI info, candidate organizational unit,
keywords, narrative, fit-rationale, anticipated uses of funding) and
explicitly addresses the four §II.F.3 EAGER appropriateness criteria
(untested/transformative, radically different approach, doesn't fit
existing programs, internal review sufficient). Year-1 go/no-go gates,
prior-feasibility evidence (Baird 2025), and a two-year ≈$279K budget
under the $300K cap are all in place. The two real risks are (a) Year-2
measurement campaigns could be misread as preliminary-data collection
(§II.F.3 exclusion), and (b) the outline lists only one candidate NSF
unit when §I.D.1 explicitly invites multiple.

## PAPPG compliance findings

### Finding 1 — Major — `pappg-24-1#II.F.3`

Section path: `Chapter II › F. Special Guidelines › 3. EAGER Proposal`
PDF page: 84
Requirement strength: required (matched: `must`, `is required`)

§II.F.3 explicitly excludes EAGER proposals "to support the collection
of preliminary data". The Year-2 paragraph in the Approach reads as a
measurement campaign — "print throughput across the farm vs. a side-by-
side baseline; print quality on a standard test artifact; UR5e/VLM
motion effects on adjacent in-progress prints; queue and retrieval
bottlenecks; and the practical gain of asynchronous over synchronous
batch operation". A program officer scanning quickly could classify
that as preliminary data on a hardware concept, which is one of the
two failure modes for EAGER triage. The Year-1 go/no-go gates already
do most of the work to dispel this — but the framing of Year 2 doesn't
re-anchor on the transformative hypothesis.

Suggested fix. Add one short sentence at the top of the Year-2
paragraph that re-anchors: e.g. "Year 2 stress-tests the *hypothesis*
(VLM-as-backbone vs. mobile-manipulator/linear-rail alternatives) under
load, not the print farm itself." Same campaigns, but they read as
falsification of the central claim rather than as throughput
characterization.

### Finding 2 — Minor — `pappg-24-1#I.D.1`

Section path: `Chapter I › D. Categories of Proposers and Proposals › 1. Concept Outlines`
PDF page: 31
Requirement strength: prohibited (matched: `not be submitted`,
`will be returned without review`)

§I.D.1 names "potentially germane NSF organizational unit(s)" — plural
— as a required field, and the corresponding ProSPCT webform allows
multiple candidates so a PO can redirect administratively rather than
by re-emailing. The draft lists only `ENG/CMMI Advanced Manufacturing`.
The "Why EAGER" paragraph itself notes the work "sits between Advanced
Manufacturing, robotics, and digital-design programs", which already
identifies at least three plausible homes.

Suggested fix. Expand the `Unit:` line to: "ENG/CMMI Advanced
Manufacturing (primary); ENG/CMMI Foundational Research in Robotics
(secondary); CISE/IIS Robust Intelligence (tertiary, for the scheduler/
generative-design layer)." This costs ~25 words, gives the recipient
PO a clean redirect path, and matches §I.D.1's explicit "unit(s)"
phrasing.

### Finding 3 — Minor — `pappg-24-1#III.A.2` / `pappg-24-1#I.D.1`

Section path: `Chapter III › A. NSF Proposal Review › 2. Merit Review Criteria`
PDF page: 110
Requirement strength: required for full proposals; permissive for
concept outlines (§I.D.1 says outlines "*may* outline … how the work
satisfies the NSB-approved Intellectual Merit and Broader Impacts
criteria")

The draft addresses both NSB criteria implicitly — "transformative if
it succeeds" (IM) and "lower the barrier to autonomous manufacturing
labs by an order of magnitude" plus the undergraduate involvement and
$<\$300K replicability (BI) — but never labels them. For a concept
outline this is permissive, not required (§I.D.1 uses *may*). For the
eventual full proposal §III.A.2 *will* require both criteria to be
fully addressed (§III.A.2: "neither, by itself, is sufficient.
Therefore, proposers must fully address both criteria").

Suggested fix (optional at concept-outline stage; required at full
proposal). Add one sentence after "Future direction": "Intellectual
merit: first quantitative engineering data on warehouse-class material-
handling hardware as a science-grade integration backbone. Broader
impacts: open hardware/software designs and an under-\$300K replication
recipe lower the barrier to autonomous manufacturing labs at PUI/R2/
new-PI institutions; the integration buyout and undergraduate-led
maintenance schedule build a workforce pipeline." ~40 words.

## What this run says about the framework

This is the second [worked example](README.md). What's different here:

1. **Different document type, no code changes required.** The framework
   was originally exercised on a Project Summary; here it runs on a
   2-page EAGER concept outline (different PAPPG chapter, different
   rule set, different audience) with the same retriever + playbook.
2. **Real draft, not a synthetic one.** Worked example #1 used a
   deliberately-broken draft to demonstrate the framework finding
   known defects. This run uses the *actual* `eager-concept-outline.tex`
   drafted on PR #10 (commit
   [cb59e56](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab-private/blob/copilot/discuss-vertical-cloud-lab-idea/eager-concept-outline.tex))
   for issue #9. The findings are real edits the author can apply.
3. **Calibrated severity.** The Project Summary review found 6 of 7
   planted defects and called them all Major. This review found 1
   Major + 2 Minors on a draft that was *not* deliberately broken —
   the framework reports findings at whatever severity the draft
   actually warrants.
4. **Token efficiency held.** Three snippets totalling ≈2,033 tokens
   covered every PAPPG provision a concept outline touches; the full
   corpus is 207k (≈100× compression).

Failure modes this run did *not* surface but a v2 should:

- The framework can't verify funding-opportunity-specific length limits
  referenced by §I.D.1 ("specific guidance on length limitations") —
  those live in DCLs and program solicitations, not PAPPG.
- The framework can't tell whether `ENG/CMMI Advanced Manufacturing` is
  *the* current cognizant unit for VCL-style work or whether a recent
  reorg moved it; that signal lives outside the document. Worth a
  human PO sanity-check before sending.
- The framework can't check the cited literature (Hung 2024, Trautt
  2024, NIST SP 1320, Baird 2025) for accuracy or currency.

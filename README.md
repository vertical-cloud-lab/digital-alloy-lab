# digital-alloy-lab

Digital alloy development at the Vertical Cloud Lab @ BYU: AI-guided design of aluminum alloys for
laser powder bed fusion (FLAIME), Bayesian optimization over function networks (BOFN), CALPHAD,
powder atomization, and the proposals built on them.

This repo was split out of the private `vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab-private` in October 2026.

- **Issues** were transferred here and kept their numbers, so old issue links redirect.
- **Open pull requests** were recreated here from the same branches, with the same numbers. Their
  review comments and commit-by-commit history stay in the private repo.
- **Closed pull requests** have a closed placeholder issue at their number, so every `#N` still
  lands on the right item.
- **Files that stay private** were left out: signed letters, per-person budget detail, CVs and similar.
  Publisher PDFs are listed by DOI in [`literature/DOIs.md`](literature/DOIs.md) instead.

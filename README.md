# digital-alloy-private

Private repository for planning and tracking the digital alloy development work at BYU Vertical Cloud Lab.

## Contents

- [Alloy Workflow Review](alloy-workflow-review.md) — Comprehensive review of the alloy synthesis, processing, characterization, and performance pipeline, including current status, equipment needs, and funding strategy. References issues and PRs in [vertical-cloud-lab/byu-vcl](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/byu-vcl).
- [Grant Targeting Analysis](grant-targeting-analysis.md) — Analysis of which grant program to target initially (NSF CAREER, DARPA DSO BAA, NSF DMR TMRP, etc.), informed by Edison Scientific literature research on program managers, funded programs, competitive landscape, key reviewers, publication venues, simulation tools, and prior literature.
- [NSF Funding Opportunities Search](nsf-funding-opportunities.md) — Comprehensive search of NSF funding opportunities (follow-up to grant targeting). Identified 10 additional programs including DMREF, Future Manufacturing, CDS&E, GOALI, and IRES. Updated to align with FLAIME / DOE Genesis Mission Topic 3B framing and current partnership structure.
- [Edison Trajectories](edison-trajectories/) — Full Edison Scientific (PaperQA3 LITERATURE_HIGH) research outputs covering program managers, funded programs, best practices, reviewers, venues, alloy discovery literature, LPBF literature, and simulation methods.

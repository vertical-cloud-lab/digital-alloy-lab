# Each bought part against what it has to fit

Checked 2026-11-09 for Sterling's request on [#180](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/pull/180): draw
every line of Ben's order list at the size its vendor publishes, so that a part that won't fit
shows up before it arrives. The pages were read with a headed Chrome under Xvfb where plain
requests were blocked (McMaster-Carr, Digi-Key, Ace). Every size used in the animation is in
[`parts_lib.py`](parts_lib.py) `DIMS`. Datasheets are linked, not copied.

**Verdicts:** ✅ fits as drawn · ⚠️ check it on the first print or on arrival · ❌ doesn't fit as the
CAD or the BOM says; the fix is in the last column.

## The graphite question

Sterling asked whether a McMaster graphite rod could replace the Pt wire. McMaster's only family
is ["Conductive Graphite Rods" (9121K)](https://www.mcmaster.com/products/graphite-rods/): 1/16, 3/32 and 1/8 in, 6 or 12 in long,
with **no purity or grade stated** and a one-sided diameter tolerance of 0 to +0.020 in (a
1/16 in rod can be 0.5 mm oversize). Edison's answer,
[`c2-01`](../copper-ded-edison/batch2/c2-01-graphite-counter-in-well.md), is summarised in
[`../copper-ded-edison/README.md`](../copper-ded-edison/README.md#batch-2-graphite-or-platinum-for-the-counter): keep the Pt coil in the cap, and
use graphite only in the bench cell.

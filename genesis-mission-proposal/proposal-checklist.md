## C. Merit Review Criteria (FOA §VI.B — in descending order of importance)

*Reviewers evaluate against these criteria. Frame every section of the narrative to score well here.*

### C.1 Criterion 1: Scientific/Technical Merit and Impact (MOST IMPORTANT)

- [ ] **AI advantage clearly articulated**: How and why does AI enhance the scientific/engineering workflows?
- [ ] **Quantitative assessment** of AI advantage trajectory (Phase I goal per FAQ)
- [ ] **Topic 3-B alignment**: AI-enabled materials discovery reducing critical mineral dependence
- [ ] **TRL advancement path**: Current TRL → Phase I exit TRL → Phase II target TRL
- [ ] **KPI improvement**: Clear path to improving key performance indicators vs. state-of-the-art
- [ ] **Novel/unique aspects highlighted**: What is genuinely new (not incremental)?
- [ ] **Transformative potential**: On trajectory to yield transformative capability or energy application approach (FAQ: "Phase I projects are expected to provide a quantitative assessment of whether the proposed approach…is on a trajectory to yield a transformative scientific capability")
- [ ] **Scaling behavior demonstrated**: Performance improves as additional data, computing, and/or other resources are applied (direct FOA language; e.g., GP surrogate MAE improvement, Pareto frontier expansion with more iterations)
- [ ] **Processability gap framing**: AI advantage measured on manufactured, tested specimens — not merely predicted compositions. FLAIME distinguishes itself from purely computational screening
- [ ] **Pre-registered benchmark protocol**: Baselines defined, random seeds specified, statistical analysis plan pre-declared before experiments begin (signals scientific rigor)

### C.2 Criterion 2: Technical Approach, Methods, and Feasibility

- [ ] **Methodology logically sound and well-justified**
- [ ] **Innovative concepts or methods** employed
- [ ] **Experimental design adequately detailed** (given 5-page constraint, prioritize clarity)
- [ ] **Potential challenges and risks identified** with reasonable mitigation strategies
- [ ] **Clear and realistic 9-month timeline** with milestones
- [ ] **6-month go/no-go** explicitly addressed in milestone table (FOA: "go/no go evaluation at 6 months"; FAQ: may request up to 3-month extension)
- [ ] **Scalability addressed** (for applied technology development)
- [ ] **Testing/validation protocols** rigorous and representative

### C.3 Criterion 3: Team, Resources, and Management

- [ ] **Team qualifications**: Well-qualified for proposed work
- [ ] **Multi-disciplinary expertise**: AI + materials science + manufacturing + characterization covered
- [ ] **Clear collaboration plan**: Balanced responsibilities, synergistic contributions, defined roles
- [ ] **Facilities adequate**: Equipment and resources appropriate (Appendix 2 supports this)
- [ ] **Data and AI model access** confirmed
- [ ] **Undergraduate team as throughput advantage**: Shift-based operation (6 days/week) enables high experiment throughput — AI selects experiments, trained operators execute them

### C.4 Criterion 4: Commercialization Potential (Applied Technology Development)

*Applies to FLAIME because Topic 3-B is under AMMTO/CMEI (applied technology)*

- [ ] **Technology transfer strategy**: Credible path beyond project duration
- [ ] **Market opportunities identified**: Preliminary assessment supporting potential impact

## F. Award Mechanism Notes

*Key details about the Other Transaction (OT) Authority mechanism used by Genesis Mission.*

- Awards issued under DOE's **Other Transaction Authority** (2 CFR 930), NOT standard grants
- Two OT formats: **(1) milestone-based** (does NOT need to comply with 2 CFR 200 cost principles) or **(2) cost-reimbursement** (similar to standard research grants under 2 CFR 200)
- Non-negotiable **"Fast-track OT"** agreements for rapid award and early start date (Webinar slide 15)
- Cost principles determined **post-selection** under 2 CFR 930.210 (RFA §III.E)
- Under milestone-based OT: greater flexibility for equipment purchases, payments tied to milestone completion
- Under cost-reimbursement OT: capital equipment (≥$5,000 per 2 CFR §200.1) may require prior written approval
- **No cost-plus awards** (FAQ); fee not applicable for non-profits
- **Computing resources**: "will not be allocated at the time of funding awards" — DOE uses a subsequent process; DOE "foresees that computing resources may be available from…National Laboratories and/or private-sector Genesis Mission partners" (Webinar slide 17)

### Post-Award Planning Items (not required at submission)

- NEPA environmental questionnaire — required after award (~$1K–$1.5K, budgeted in budget-breakdown-phase1.md)
- Data Management and Sharing Plan (DMSP) — required during award negotiation
- IP/Data Rights provisions — will be specified in the OT agreement post-selection

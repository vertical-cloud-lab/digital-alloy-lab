## Why this is Tier 3 but the TRUE long pole

BYU's Letter of Commitment (from SPO / Dean / authorized university official)
is the only Appendix 7 item I can't draft-and-send in a single day — **SPO
requires a routed request with budget, narrative, and partner structure
first**. Typical turnaround is 5–10 business days. Start this today, even
before the other outreach is done, or it becomes the submission blocker.

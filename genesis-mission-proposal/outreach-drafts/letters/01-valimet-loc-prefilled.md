Dear Dr. Baird:

This letter confirms the commitment of Valimet, Inc. (Stockton, California)
to participate as a **funded subrecipient under a subaward from
Brigham Young University (BYU, prime)** in the FLAIME project submitted
to the DOE Genesis Mission RFA (DE-FOA-0003612, Topic 3-B: AI-autonomous
discovery of critical-mineral-lean aluminum alloys for additive
manufacturing).

**Scope.** Valimet will provide gas-atomized aluminum alloy powder
production for compositions selected by FLAIME's BOFN (Bayesian
optimization with function networks) discovery engine, delivering
20–63 µm powder at [Lot size kg] kg per composition across approximately
[# of compositions] scale-up runs over the 9-month Phase I period of
performance, on a quarterly cadence gated to FLAIME experimental
milestones. Detailed scope, deliverables, and acceptance criteria are
provided in the attached **Scope of Work** (`02-valimet-scope-of-work.md`).

**Key personnel and facility.** Valimet commits the following named key
personnel and facility to FLAIME for the full Phase I period of
performance:

  - **Valimet's CTO** — significant
    intellectual leadership per FOA §II.B as **co-developer of the
    gas-atomization scale-up envelope**: joint definition of process
    windows for the production-scale runs, joint specification of powder
    acceptance criteria, contribution to the manufacturability gates that
    feed back into the BOFN loop, and BOFN-loop coordination with the BYU PI.
  - **Valimet's technical manager** — atomization run execution,
    powder characterization (PSD, oxygen, ICP), and lot release.
  - **Valimet's gas-atomization facility, Stockton, California** —
    dedicated atomizer capacity for FLAIME lots, on-site PSD and
    chemistry QC, and powder traceability/retain-sample handling.

**Cost share.** Consistent with FOA §II.B (for-profit cost-share
requirement), Valimet commits the FOA-required cost-share contribution
[amount removed from the public copy] for Phase I. The cost-share contribution will
be provided **primarily as in-kind** support — including allocated
atomizer-hours at Valimet's loaded run rate, engineering and technician
labor at fully-burdened rates, powder characterization runs (PSD, ICP,
oxygen), and allocated facility/utility overhead — documented and
audit-defensible per 2 CFR 200.306. Detail and valuation methodology
are provided in the attached **Budget Justification** and **Cost-Share
Declaration**.

**Execution.** The scope, budget, deliverables, and milestones above
will be executed in accordance with the FLAIME Project Narrative and
the BYU subaward agreement to be issued upon DOE award. Valimet will
participate in DOE-aligned open data sharing for composition and process
metadata as described in the FLAIME Data Management Plan, with
proprietary process know-how protected under a mutual NDA to be put in
place at subaward execution.

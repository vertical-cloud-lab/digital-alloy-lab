## Format — use the DOE biosketch (3 pages max)

The Genesis Mission RFA (§IV.C) **prefers the DOE biosketch format**
(3 pages max per individual) over the NIH format (5 pages max). Please
use the DOE format. Sections:

- **(a) Education and Training** — degrees, institutions, fields, dates.
- **(b) Research and Professional Experience** — positions held in
  reverse chronological order, with organization, title, and dates.
- **(c) Publications and Patents** — up to **10 most relevant** items
  (peer-reviewed papers, patents, conference proceedings, technical
  reports). Atomization / Al-alloy / AM-feedstock relevance preferred.
- **(d) Synergistic Activities** — up to **5** items (standards
  committees, editorial boards, professional society leadership,
  outreach, mentorship of early-career staff, AS9100 / NADCAP audit
  leadership, etc.).

> If a DOE biosketch is genuinely impractical for either individual,
> the NIH biosketch (5 pages max) is an acceptable fallback per common
> federal practice; please flag this to BYU OSP if used.

## Submission

- One **PDF** per person.
- Filename convention: **`[LastName]-biosketch.pdf`**
  → e.g., `CTO-biosketch.pdf`, `technical-manager-biosketch.pdf`.
- Email both PDFs to the BYU OSP point of contact listed in the cover letter.

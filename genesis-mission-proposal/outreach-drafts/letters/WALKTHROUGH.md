# Valimet Subaward Packet — 7-Day Walk-Through

*Read this second (after `FAQ.md`). This is a step-by-step timeline assigning each task to a Valimet role. Total estimated effort: **~5–6 hours of Valimet staff time across roles**, spread over 7 calendar days. None of the steps require anything you don't already do for other federal subawards.*

**Roles referenced:**
- **Authorized Signatory** — officer empowered to bind Valimet (typically CEO/President or designee).
- **Contracts** — contracts/compliance lead (handles SAM.gov, Reps & Certs, foreign-connections form).
- **Technical Lead** — Valimet's CTO and/or Valimet's technical manager.
- **Finance** — controller / finance manager (handles indirect-rate election, cost-share valuation, budget).
- **HR** — for biosketch / key-personnel info (often Tech Lead can supply).

---

## Day 0 — Receive packet
- [ ] **Action:** Open `00-cover-letter.md`, then `FAQ.md`, then this file. Forward the packet to Contracts, Finance, and the Authorized Signatory so all four roles know it's incoming.
- **Asset:** `00-cover-letter.md`, `FAQ.md`, `WALKTHROUGH.md`
- **Owner:** Technical Lead — distribution only
- **Time:** ~15 min

## Day 1 — SAM.gov UEI verification + entity active status
- [ ] **Action:** Confirm Valimet's **Unique Entity ID (UEI)** at SAM.gov, verify **registration is "Active"** and not within 60 days of expiration, and copy the UEI + CAGE code into the subrecipient profile section of `07-valimet-coi-reps.md`. If registration is expiring soon, renew now — DOE will not issue an award to a lapsed entity, and renewal can take 7–10 business days.
- **Asset:** `07-valimet-coi-reps.md` (subrecipient profile fields)
- **Owner:** Contracts
- **Time:** ~30 min

## Day 2 — Sign Letter of Commitment + cost-share commitment
- [ ] **Action:** Review the pre-filled Letter of Commitment for accuracy (named PI, scope summary, dollar figures, cost-share line). Authorized Signatory signs. Technical Lead reviews the scope summary for technical accuracy before signing. Return as a signed PDF (in the final return on Day 7).
- **Asset:** `01-valimet-loc-prefilled.md`, `02-valimet-scope-of-work.md`, `03-valimet-cost-share-guide.md`
- **Owner:** Authorized Signatory (signs) + Technical Lead (technical review)
- **Time:** ~1 hr

## Day 3 — DOE Transparency of Foreign Connections form
- [ ] **Action:** Complete the DOE Transparency of Foreign Connections (TFC) disclosure for the listed key personnel. The walk-through doc explains each section; for a US-based for-profit with no foreign affiliations, **most fields will be "Nothing to Report."** Don't skip the form on that basis — DOE requires a positive "Nothing to Report" entry, not a blank.
- **Asset:** `06-valimet-tfc-guide.md`
- **Owner:** Contracts / Compliance
- **Time:** ~1 hr

## Day 4 — Subrecipient profile, COI disclosure, Reps & Certs
- [ ] **Action:** Fill the subrecipient profile (entity info, UEI, CAGE, NAICS, prior federal awards summary), the conflict-of-interest disclosure for key personnel, and the Reps & Certs checklist (debarment, lobbying, anti-trafficking, etc.). All standard federal forms; checklist format makes this fast.
- **Asset:** `07-valimet-coi-reps.md`
- **Owner:** Contracts
- **Time:** ~30 min

## Day 5 — Budget justification + indirect-rate election + cost-share worksheet
- [ ] **Action:** Populate the budget template line by line (labor by person and % effort, materials, equipment time, travel if any, indirects). **Elect either Valimet's NICRA or 15% de minimis MTDC** (de minimis rate raised from 10% → 15% effective 2024-10-01) (one or the other — see FAQ). Complete the cost-share worksheet to document the in-kind contribution with valuation basis for each line item.
- **Asset:** `05-valimet-budget-justification.md`, `04-valimet-cost-share-worksheet.md`, `03-valimet-cost-share-guide.md` (reference)
- **Owner:** Finance (with Tech Lead input on staff effort and atomizer hours)
- **Time:** ~1 hr

## Day 6 — Key-personnel biosketches (×2) + facilities description
- [ ] **Action:** Provide DOE-format biosketches (≤2 pages each) for the two named key personnel (typically Valimet's CTO and technical manager or designee), plus a 1–2 page facilities & equipment description covering the rePowder atomizer suite, characterization capabilities, and any relevant quality systems (ISO/AS certifications). Templates are in the request docs.
- **Asset:** `09-valimet-key-personnel-request.md`, `10-valimet-facilities-request.md`
- **Owner:** Technical Lead + HR
- **Time:** ~1 hr

## Day 7 — Internal review and return signed packet to BYU OSP
- [ ] **Action:** PI/CTO + Authorized Signatory do a final read-through to confirm internal consistency (dollar figures match across LoC, budget, and cost-share worksheet; named personnel match across biosketches and budget). Compile the completed forms into a single signed PDF packet (or a zipped folder of signed PDFs) and email to BYU OSP per instructions in `README.md`. Cc Sterling Baird (sterling_baird@byu.edu).
- **Asset:** `README.md`, `11-valimet-byu-osp-mechanics.md`
- **Owner:** Authorized Signatory + Technical Lead
- **Time:** ~30 min

---

**Total estimated effort: ~5–6 hours of Valimet staff time across roles** (Signatory ~1 hr, Contracts ~2 hr, Technical Lead ~1.5 hr, Finance ~1 hr, HR <30 min).

---

### What happens after we receive your packet

BYU OSP and the FLAIME PI team will integrate the Valimet materials (LoC, scope, budget, cost-share worksheet, biosketches, facilities, TFC, COI/Reps&Certs) into the prime proposal package and submit to DOE before the FOA deadline. You'll receive a confirmation email with the DOE submission receipt. If DOE makes the award, BYU OSP will issue a formal FDP-template subaward agreement to Valimet (typically within 30–60 days of the federal award notice), at which point invoicing and reporting per `11-valimet-byu-osp-mechanics.md` begin. Until the subaward agreement is fully executed, no costs may be incurred against the project at Valimet's risk unless pre-award costs are explicitly authorized in writing.

# BYU OSP Subaward Mechanics — How the Valimet Subaward Will Operate

*One-pager for Valimet contracts and finance staff. Describes how the BYU → Valimet subaward will be issued, invoiced, reported on, and modified once DOE makes the prime award.*

---

## BYU's role as prime

BYU is the prime recipient on the DOE Genesis Mission Phase I award (FOA `DE-FOA-0003612`, Topic 3-B). Upon DOE award notification, **BYU's Office of Sponsored Programs (OSP) will issue a formal subaward agreement to Valimet** that flows down the applicable DOE Other Transaction Agreement (OTA) terms, Bayh-Dole obligations, Uniform Guidance (2 CFR 200) requirements, and FOA-specific conditions. No costs may be incurred against the project at Valimet until the subaward agreement is fully executed (unless pre-award costs are explicitly authorized in writing by BYU OSP).

## Subaward agreement template

BYU OSP uses the **Federal Demonstration Partnership (FDP) Subaward Template** as the base instrument. The FDP template is the standard across U.S. research universities, well known to most institutional contracting officers, and minimizes negotiation cycles. Attachments include: scope of work (from `02-valimet-scope-of-work.md`), budget (from `05-valimet-budget-justification.md`), cost-share commitment (from `04-valimet-cost-share-worksheet.md`), and a flow-down terms attachment listing the DOE-specific clauses that apply to Valimet as subrecipient.

## Invoicing

- **Cadence:** Monthly cost-reimbursement.
- **Terms:** Net-30 from receipt of a clean invoice.
- **Recipient:** BYU OSP subaward officer (`[BYU_OSP_SUBAWARDS_OFFICER TBD — PI fill]`, `[email TBD]`).
- **Backup required:** Labor (person, hours, rate, % effort), materials (vendor, PO, amount), equipment time (atomizer hours with date), travel (if any), and indirect-cost calculation showing the elected rate applied to the correct base.
- **Advance payment:** Not provided unless specifically negotiated and documented in the subaward agreement (advance payment to for-profit subrecipients requires additional justification under 2 CFR 200).

## Audit thresholds

Per **Uniform Guidance §200.501** (formerly OMB Circular A-133), a non-federal entity that **expends ≥$750K in federal funds in a fiscal year** must obtain a Single Audit. This subaward by itself would not trigger this; if Valimet is already in a Single Audit regime from other federal funding, the existing audit will cover this subaward as well. Below the threshold, BYU's annual subrecipient monitoring questionnaire and Valimet's normal accounting records are sufficient. Cost-share documentation (timesheets, equipment-time logs, valuation memos) must be retained **three years after final project closeout**.

## Modifications

Any change to **scope, budget (line-item reallocation >25%), period of performance, or key personnel** must be processed through **BYU OSP → DOE Contracting Officer**. The PI cannot unilaterally modify the subaward. Workflow: Valimet sends a written change request (with rationale and revised budget/scope as needed) to the BYU OSP subaward officer; BYU OSP packages and submits to the DOE CO; once DOE approves, BYU OSP issues a subaward modification to Valimet. Verbal agreements with the PI do **not** constitute approved modifications. Minor changes (e.g., shifting a deliverable due date by ≤30 days within the same budget category) can typically be handled at the PI/OSP level without DOE action — when in doubt, ask.

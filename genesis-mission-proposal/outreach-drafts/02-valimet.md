=== Valimet scope in FLAIME ===

Gas atomization of selected Al alloy compositions drawn from FLAIME's BOFN
(Bayesian optimization with function networks) pipeline. Specifically:

  - ~6 scale-up runs over Phase I (with sensitivity for 1–2 lots upsized
    to 75 kg if a hit composition warrants)
  - Target PSD: 20–63 µm (LPBF spec)
  - Lot size: ≥50 kg per composition (default)
  - Schedule: quarterly lots, gated on FLAIME experimental milestones;
    we coordinate ≥60 days ahead of each run
  - Compositions: Al-based, critical-mineral-lean (low Mn/Cr/Co)

Full detail in the enclosed `02-valimet-scope-of-work.md`. If any of this
needs to flex to fit your atomizer schedule or staffing, tell me now and
we'll rebalance before the LoC is signed.

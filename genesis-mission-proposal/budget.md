## F. Other Direct Costs

### Materials and Supplies

* **Research-scale elemental feedstock:** $35,354
  (sized from powder throughput and build geometry across both in-house and partner LPBF systems. At BYU, builds are performed on the **Mastrex MX-120** (120 mm diameter), co-located with the ultrasonic atomizer (see letter of collaboration). At UofU, builds are performed on the **Aconity MIDI** (170 mm diameter × 200 mm height). Assuming ~5 mm effective part height and 3× hopper fill:

  * MX-120: area ≈113 cm² → part volume ≈56 cm³ → powder ≈170 cm³ → ≈220 g/build (1.3 g/cm³ apparent density (≈1.6 g/cm³ tap))
  * Aconity MIDI: area ≈227 cm² → part volume ≈113 cm³ → powder ≈340 cm³ → ≈440 g/build (1.3 g/cm³ apparent density (≈1.6 g/cm³ tap))

  Using a blended estimate (~300 g/build) over ~320 builds yields ≈96 kg powder demand; applying ~2× factor for losses, recycling inefficiency, and failed prints gives ≈190 kg total material demand. A substantial fraction of the aluminum in each melt (≈50–70%) is provided by **high-purity machined Al crucibles** (see separate line item below), with the remainder supplied by elemental additions and supplemental Al shot. Effective blended material cost (~$150–$200/kg) supports the request; adjusted for actual 39-week PoP (16 compositions/week × 39 weeks = 624 total capacity).)

* **Gas-atomization feedstock (Valimet ingot material):** $25,000
  (6 distinct ingot compositions × 500 lb each = 3,000 lb total; $3–$10/lb range corresponds to $9,000–$30,000 total; $25,000 used as a realistic midpoint for budgeting.)

* **Inert gas (4.5N-grade Ar, Ar-rich feed):** $6,000
  (per-run usage follows induction atomization workflow: multi-stage purge (~400–500 L), process flow (~150 L), pour/boost phase (~75 L), cooldown (~25 L) ≈650–750 L Ar/run. Over ~160 atomization runs (covering screened compositions plus baseline, repeatability, and contingency runs), this is ~120 m³ of Ar covered by ~16 K-size 4.5N-Ar cylinder exchanges at ~$210/exchange, plus cylinder rental over 9 months on two on-site cylinders, and regulator/check-valve/leak-test contingency.)

* **High-purity machined Al crucibles:** $3,000
  (machined in-house from 99.9–99.99% Al bar stock; contributes ~50–70% of melt mass per run and is explicitly accounted for in feedstock totals. Cost reflects small-lot procurement and machining scrap.)

* **Sonotrode tip replacement contingency:** $2,500
  (covers non-standard wear or damage during experimental campaigns beyond nominal lifetime.)

* **LPBF consumables:** $4,000
  (build plates, recoater blades, filters, powder handling supplies, sieves, and minor maintenance.)

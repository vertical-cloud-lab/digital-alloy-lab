# Provenance of powder lot k7m2q9 (630 bronze, Oct 27, 2026)

`k7m2q9` is the first copper-alloy powder from the lab's AMAZEMET rePOWDER: 630 bronze (UNS C63000,
a nickel-aluminum bronze) atomized on 2026-10-27 ([#219](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/issues/219),
row 6 of [`../runs.csv`](../runs.csv)). Each step gives what the records say and the file, photo or
video behind it. **No record** marks what nobody has written down yet. This restates the records as of
2026-11-25; nothing here is a new measurement.

> **The code no longer means one run.** On Oct 29 the Oct 27 and Oct 28 powder was sieved together,
> and the sieved jars are labelled `k7m2q9`. Only the SEM sample, taken on Oct 27, is Oct 27 powder alone.
> The run log and the video title say "Cu70/Ni30"; the charge was 630 bronze
> ([correction](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/issues/219#issuecomment-6460235121)).

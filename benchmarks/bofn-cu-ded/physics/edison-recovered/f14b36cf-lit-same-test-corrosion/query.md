CONTEXT (do not summarize this back): a synthetic benchmark for Bayesian optimization of copper alloys
for blown-powder laser DED (ONR YIP proposal; EXCLUDE publications by Sterling G. Baird). Composition box (wt%, Cu balance):
Ni 0-30, Al 0-9.5, Fe 0-4.5, Mn 0-4. A candidate passes if its 30-day seawater (or ASTM D1141 / 3.5% NaCl) immersion
mass loss is no greater than that of wrought 70-30 Cu-Ni (C71500) in the same test. The benchmark currently uses a
hand-written function under which nothing with less than about 6 wt% Ni can pass. We want to replace it with a simple
mixed-potential electrochemical model (oxygen-reduction cathode, copper dissolution through a growing Ni/Fe-doped
Cu2O film, an Al2O3-rich layer for aluminum-containing alloys, and selective dissolution of retained beta-prime)
whose constants come from measurements. Give DOIs, alloy compositions, processing, electrolyte, temperature, flow,
exposure time and the numbers. Say plainly when no measurement exists.

QUESTION: Which published studies measured the corrosion of several of these alloy families IN THE SAME TEST
(same electrolyte, exposure time and laboratory), for exposures of about 1 to 90 days: copper, Cu-Ni with different
nickel levels (e.g. 5, 10, 20, 30 wt% Ni, with and without 1-2% Fe), aluminum bronzes without nickel (e.g. C61400,
CuAl7Fe2-3), nickel-aluminum bronze (C95800/C63000, cast, annealed, welded or additively made), and Cu-Ni-Al alloys
(MARINEL, Hiduron, Cu-7Ni-xAl)? For each, give the mass-loss rate or i_corr at each time and the ranking. Is there
measured evidence for a minimum nickel content needed to match 70-30 Cu-Ni in a 30-day test, and do aluminum bronzes
or NAB without retained beta match or beat 70-30 Cu-Ni in the same test? How does the ranking at 1 hour compare with
the ranking at 30 days (film maturation)?

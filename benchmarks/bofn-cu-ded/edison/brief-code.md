Independent correctness review of a cost-aware Bayesian optimization benchmark: pKGFN (partial knowledge gradient for function networks, Buathong et al., ICML 2024) on a synthetic copper-alloy function network for blown-powder directed energy deposition (DED). Please act as a skeptical reviewer who is also willing to run code.

The uploaded files are the complete benchmark: bofn_alloy.py (synthetic truth, GP, network model, the KG estimator, the lab that charges for each stage), policies.py (pKGFN and its variants, and every baseline), run_sweep.py, check_truth.py (required properties of the truth), analyze.py, plot.py, README.md (the claims as written), truth_tables.md, settings.md (versions, settings, costs, the required properties, true optima), and results.zip (every run finished at upload time, as JSON). Read settings.md first. It is a port of an aluminum benchmark that an earlier review of yours checked; the copper network has eight priced stages instead of four (CALPHAD and heat flow $10; a 0.5 kg ultrasonic powder lot $400; small DED at Utah $500 with laser power capped at 0.5; large DED at Mines $3,100 for the first session on a lot, which buys a 2 kg scale-up, then $1,500 per session for up to four sessions; microscopy $100; tensile $200; a one-hour corrosion screen $50; a 30-day immersion $400), an objective gated by immersion, J = U * sigmoid((m - m_ref)/0.05) - 0.3 B, and two bias knobs (kappa: the CALPHAD crack screen misses the iron-rich nucleant; eta: the one-hour screen misses selective attack of beta-prime).

The claim under review will be the README's "Bottom line" at (kappa, eta) = (1, 1), 20 seeds: how much less money pKGFN and BOFN with lot reuse need than tiered screening to reach tiered screening's $100k level. The runs may still be incomplete at upload; review the code first.

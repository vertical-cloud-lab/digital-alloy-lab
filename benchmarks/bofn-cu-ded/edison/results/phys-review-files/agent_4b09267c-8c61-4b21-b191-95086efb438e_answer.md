### Results and limitations

- **CICEQ 2021 retrieved successfully:** Tables 1–2 supply five composition-linked corrosion-current observations and **four Cu-referenced contrasts**. Exact icorr values are Cu **13.95**, Cu-Al **6.56**, Cu-Al-Ni **5.17**, Cu-Al-Mn **3.48**, and Cu-Al-Mn-Ni **2.83 µA/cm²**, in 0.5 M NaCl at 20 °C. Conditioning was **60 minutes OCP followed by EIS of unspecified duration**, then polarization—not a precisely one-hour exposure. These four contrasts share the Cu control.
- **Tropical 16-year report retrieved successfully:** Original **NRL 6452, 28 October 1966**, via the DTIC-contributed Internet Archive mirror. Exact printed average penetrations support **25 matched Cu/aluminium-bronze contrasts**, covering five environments and 1, 2, 4, 8, 16 years. Rates are explicitly derived as printed cumulative penetration divided by years. **Immersion bronze contains 5.07 wt% Al; atmospheric bronze contains 3.77 wt% Al.** They were not conflated.
- **Dhar 1985, DOI 10.5006/1.3582011:** metadata and institutional abstracts retrieved, but publisher article/PDF access returned HTTP403 and alternate parsing failed. The Cu/90Cu-10Ni/70Cu-30Ni scope is verified, but exact rates, analytical compositions and durations remain unavailable. **No numeric rows were invented; whether useful rates are tabled or figure-only remains unverified.**

The usable additions connect **pure Cu to Al-bearing alloys**, including Cu-Al-Ni. They do **not** recover a direct Cu5Ni-to-binary-Cu10Ni/Cu30Ni bridge. No graph digitization or model fitting was performed. Reported means lack usable SD/SE/CI; no uncertainty values were fabricated.

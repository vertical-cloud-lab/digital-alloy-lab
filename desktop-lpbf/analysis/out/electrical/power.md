## 3. UPS (Eaton 5PX1500RTG2, 1,500 W) through a power cut

Backed load: 770 W rated, 241 W while building, 63 W held after the PC and servos shut down (safety controller, guard lock, PLC, O2 analyser, Pi, switch). Battery 432 Wh nominal (battery 4 x 12 V 9 Ah assumed for the 5PX 1500 G2 class (not read from Eaton's datasheet: its page timed out); Peukert k 1.15 for VRLA; inverter 88 % (byu-vcl PR 318's Vertiv figure). ENERGY STAR's listing of the 5PX1500RTG2 (read 2026-11-23 22:48Z) gives 7 min at 100 % (1,500 W) and 14 min at 50 % load: about 175 Wh delivered at both, which the Peukert model is checked against below).

| Case | Runtime (min) |

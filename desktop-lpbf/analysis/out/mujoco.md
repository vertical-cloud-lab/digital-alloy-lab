Released just past that, the door (1.396 kg) reaches a peak angular speed 13.45 rad/s (free edge 1.68 m/s); peak stop torque 13.7 N·m at t = 0.521 s. contacts off, hinge friction and damping zero (worst case); MuJoCo's soft joint limit stands in for a hard stop, so the stop torque depends on its stiffness.

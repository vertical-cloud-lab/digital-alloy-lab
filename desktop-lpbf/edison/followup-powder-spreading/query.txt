We will spread gas-atomised aluminium alloy powders (AlSi10Mg and Al-Mg-Sc-Zr, particle size about 20-63 um, D50 about 35-40 um) with a rigid high-speed-steel blade in 30-50 um layers onto 25-75 mm diameter build plates, dosed from above by a fluted roller with a dose factor of about 1.5-2. We plan discrete element method (DEM) simulations (LIGGGHTS, Hertz-Mindlin with simplified JKR cohesion) and want realistic inputs and benchmarks. Please review the literature with citations and quantitative values:
1. DEM parameters used for Al alloy powders in spreading simulations: Young's modulus (and scaled values), Poisson ratio, restitution coefficient, sliding and rolling friction coefficients, surface energy or cohesion energy density for AlSi10Mg and other Al powders, and how they were calibrated (angle of repose, Hall or Carney flow, rotating drum or Revolution powder analyser, shear cell).
2. Results of spreading studies: packing fraction and coverage of the spread layer against the ratio of layer thickness or blade gap to D90, blade speed, blade type (rigid, soft, roller), and the critical gap below which jamming, streaks or empty patches appear. The effective powder layer thickness in steady state after melt shrinkage, compared with the nominal layer thickness.
3. Cohesion metrics for 20-63 um Al powders: granular Bond number, Hausner ratio, avalanche angle, and the effects of humidity, surface oxide and drying.
4. Dose factor recommendations and the effect of short feed; hopper flow of fine Al powder: bridging and ratholing, minimum outlet widths (Jenike) for slots of about 8 mm, and wall angles for mass flow in stainless hoppers.
5. Powder migration into gaps around build plates and pistons (radial clearances of 0.1-0.4 mm) and the seals used to prevent it.

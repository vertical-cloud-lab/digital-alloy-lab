| Thermography or pyrometry | Multiple LPBF/DED alloys; no aluminium hot-crack study in the available evidence directly imaged crack initiation thermally | Typical Si NIR: approximately 0.4–1.0 µm; InGaAs SWIR: approximately 0.95–1.7 µm; two-colour high-speed imaging examples used about 700 and 950 nm | Coaxial for the melt pool or off-axis for the track tail, heat-affected zone and layer-scale heat accumulation | Usually correlated with metallography, CT, process parameters or thermal models rather than direct crack-opening observation | Measures the causal environment—temperature field, thermal gradient, cooling rate, remelting and heat accumulation—not the crack itself. Two-colour Ti6Al4V imaging measured roughly 1–40 K/µs cooling and 5–20 K/µm gradients, but emissivity, plume attenuation, reflections and geometry can make temperature estimates misleading, particularly for aluminium. Treat it as a crack-susceptibility covariate unless independently validated. (pqac-00000033, pqac-00000034, pqac-00000055) |

What have groups that built their own laser powder bed fusion (LPBF / SLM) machines reported as lessons learned, design mistakes, commissioning problems and maintenance burden? Include open-architecture and open-source research machines (for example NIST AMMT, Heriot-Watt's open-architecture LPBF, Sheffield, Fraunhofer ILT and Aachen research machines, Penn State, Lawrence Livermore, Carnegie Mellon, open-hardware papers in HardwareX or Additive Manufacturing, LPBF testbeds, small LPBF set-ups for alloy screening, and in-situ X-ray LPBF replicators used at synchrotrons such as the APS, SLS and ESRF). Report, with citations and numbers where available: time and cost to build; what took longest to commission; what failed; how they handled laser safety, gas purity, powder handling, recoating, alignment and calibration (focus, scanner field correction, laser power calibration), and software and controls; and what they would do differently. Also include published experience with commercial desktop or small-format LPBF systems used in research labs (for example TRUMPF TruPrint 1000, Sisma MYSINT100, OR Laser ORLAS Creator, Farsoon FS121M, Xact Metal XM200, One Click Metal MPRINT, AO Metal, Aconity MINI and MIDI, DMG MORI Lasertec 12 SLM): maintenance, filter changes, the effort to change over between alloys, and problems reported.

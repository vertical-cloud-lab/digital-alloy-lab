For a 6-10 L laser powder bed fusion (LPBF) process chamber of bare 6061-T6 aluminium with FKM O-ring face seals (about 2.3 m of seal in total), KF-25 vacuum flanges, a sliding build-piston seal, an O-ring gland on the piston rod and a rotary shaft feedthrough for a recoater, vacuum-purged to about 1 mbar, back-filled with argon 5.0 and run at +5 to +20 mbar with a recirculating blower and HEPA filter, please review the literature with citations and quantitative values:
1. Residual O2 and H2O levels LPBF machines achieve with vacuum purging versus flow purging; the number of purge cycles or gas volumes needed; typical leak rates (mbar L/s) and rate-of-rise tests for such chambers; measured values for research machines (NIST AMMT, Aconity, open-source machines) and commercial machines (EOS, Renishaw, SLM, TRUMPF) where published.
2. O2 and moisture thresholds recommended for processing Al alloys (AlSi10Mg, Al-Mg-Sc-Zr, 7xxx) and how O2 at 100-2000 ppm and the dew point affect porosity, spatter, oxide inclusions, hydrogen porosity and fatigue.
3. Seal design for vacuum plus low positive pressure: O-ring squeeze, gland fill and surface finish; permeation of O2 and H2O through FKM compared with other elastomers; outgassing of FKM and aluminium walls and the effect of a 200 C build plate warming the chamber; dynamic seals for build-piston rods and recoater shafts (O-ring glands, PTFE lip seals, edge-welded bellows, magnetic-fluid feedthroughs) with their leak rates and wear; sealing build pistons against powder (felt, PTFE, brush, labyrinth).
4. Oxygen sensors for LPBF: zirconia, electrochemical/galvanic and optical; accuracy between 1 and 1000 ppm, response time, poisoning by metal condensate or silicones, cross-sensitivity to hydrogen, and calibration practice; dew-point sensors.
5. Moisture carried by Al alloy powders (ppm or wt%), drying practice (temperature, time, vacuum), and the effect on chamber dew point and on part hydrogen porosity.

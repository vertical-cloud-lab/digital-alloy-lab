Include an oxide-condition comparison and neutral-density attenuation test applied equally and then unequally to the two bands. |
| 3 | AE crack signal buried by gas-loop/mechanical/EMI noise | In an LPBF crack-monitoring study, activating the inert-gas circulation pump raised AE noise from approximately **0.2 V to 2 V**—a tenfold amplitude increase.

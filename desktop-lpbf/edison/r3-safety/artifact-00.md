| Hazard / subsystem | Evidence or design basis | Design-review proposal / acceptance criterion |
|---|---|---|
| **Laser enclosure / guard** | A historical IEC 60825-4 committee report suggested passive-guard test classes **T1 = 30,000 s**, **T2 = 100 s**, and **T3 = 10 s**; these are not universal guarantees under the current standard. Testing used worst-case credible exposure and required rear-side emission below the Class 1 AEL. At 500 W, the corresponding incident energies are **15 MJ, 50 kJ, and 5 kJ**. No blanket survivability time for aluminium or steel sheet follows from these values. (pqac-00000022) | Make normal operation **Class 1** by validated enclosure testing. Use a two-channel monitored, guard-locking interlock; safety shutter/attenuator; laser-source inhibit; scanner safe-torque-off; captured beam dump; and manual reset outside the hazard zone. Qualify every wall, seam, aperture, window, dump, and likely aluminium back-reflection path at worst-case focus, fault position, beam diameter, power, and response time. Treat service with containment defeated as **Class 4** under an LSO-approved SOP and NHZ. |
| **Oxygen / argon** | OSHA treats **<19.5% O₂** as oxygen-deficient/IDLH. A NIST LPBF installation warned below 19% and evacuated below 17%; it also recommended fixed room monitoring, argon shutoff, ventilation, and outdoor exhaust. (pqac-00000016, pqac-00000023, pqac-00000065) | Install independent O₂ sensors in the room and inside the lift enclosure, positioned from a dispersion assessment—not merely “low because argon is heavy.” Proposed conservative controls: **warning at 20.0%**, automatic argon isolation and high ventilation no later than **19.5%**, and evacuation/latching alarm below 19.5%. Interlock laser and purge operation to ventilation and monitor health; provide pressure regulation, secured cylinders, check valves, relief devices, and safe outdoor discharge. |
| **Metal powders** | One AlSi10Mg test reported **Pmax 0.61 MPa (6.1 bar)**, **Kst 15.10 MPa·m/s (151 bar·m/s)**, and **MIE 80 mJ**; values depend strongly on particle-size distribution, oxidation, dispersion, and sample history. (pqac-00000019, pqac-00000025, pqac-00000071) | Complete a combustible-dust DHA using representative virgin, reused, spilled, condensate, cyclone, and filter samples—not the published values alone. Minimize powder inventory and clouds; use conductive/bonded transfer paths, verified grounding, non-sparking tools, conductive footwear/flooring, closed containers, combustible-dust-rated vacuum equipment, exposure monitoring, and a medically managed respirator program. |
| **Cyclone / HEPA filter** | Cyclones enrich the downstream filter in finer dust. Filter-collected aluminium near **D50 ≈ 5 μm** has measured **MIE <3 mJ**, and filter Kst can be **2–3 times** that of bulk feed. Unpassivated LPBF condensate may ignite when air enters during maintenance. Wet systems suppress clouds but aluminium/water contact can generate hydrogen. (pqac-00000017, pqac-00000018) | Do not accept an ordinary HEPA unit as explosion protection. Use a metal-dust-qualified, process-specific inerted dry collector or engineered wet collector after compatibility review. Provide isolation, grounding, differential-pressure/temperature monitoring, safe venting or suppression where required, and an inert filter-change/passivation method. Filter changes require shutdown/LOTO, restricted access, two trained people, fire-resistant PPE, controlled air admission, and approved hazardous-waste packaging. |
| **Thermal / vacuum** | The proposed plate is **200 °C**, substantially hotter than the 80 °C NIST platform that required heat-resistant gloves. Pump-down to about 1 mbar exposes the chamber and viewport to essentially **one atmosphere differential pressure**; controlled-atmosphere window guidance requires design for maximum credible vacuum, fire, impact, deterioration, and positive pressure where applicable. (pqac-00000015, pqac-00000043, pqac-00000044) | Guard and label accessible hot surfaces; use independent over-temperature sensing and a safety contactor, cooldown confirmation before robotic or manual exchange, heat-rated grippers, and a noncombustible landing station. Engineer the chamber, door, seals, and laser-rated viewport for full vacuum plus maximum positive pressure and faults; add protective retention/shielding, inspection criteria, vacuum/pressure relief, door pressure-equalization interlock, and proof documentation. |
| **Robot / mobile base / lift** | ISO 10218-1:2025 addresses robot design; ISO 10218-2:2025 addresses integrated applications/cells. ISO/TS 15066 supplies collaborative modes and contact criteria. ANSI/RIA R15.08 Part 1 covers mobile-robot manufacturers and Part 2 system integrators. “Cobot” status does not make the combined arm, payload, hot plate, drawer, machine, MiR, and lift application inherently safe. (pqac-00000054, pqac-00000059, pqac-00000062) | Perform one ISO 12100 lifecycle risk assessment covering docking error, dropped/hot loads, crushing and trapping, sharp parts, powder release, lift motion, unexpected restart, communications loss, and recovery/service. Require safety-rated docking and tray-position confirmation plus a **mobile interlock**: laser shutter closed and emission inhibited, chamber equalized/cool, lift and machine motion stopped, and robot/MiR in verified safe state before access. Coordinate E-stops and STO across all subsystems; validate stopping distances, payload/gripper retention, collaborative limits, reset locations, and prevention of automatic restart. |

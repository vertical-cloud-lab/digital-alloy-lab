Beam path: a 500 W single-mode CW Yb fibre laser at 1070 nm (IPG YLR-500-U3-SM-AC, M2 1.05-1.1, QBH-compatible output, 5 m fibre) into IPG's standard collimator (5 mm or 7.5 mm collimated beam), then a SCANLAB basiCube 20 galvo (20 mm aperture, mirrors rated about 1 kW at 1064 nm without cooling), then a fused-silica f-theta lens with f = 160 mm (Thorlabs FTH160-1064 class) or f = 254 mm, then an AR-coated fused-silica chamber window (Thorlabs WG41050-C class, 2 in.) with a replaceable 3 mm fused-silica cover slide below it, then a 25-75 mm diameter heated (200 C) build plate in argon at +5 to +20 mbar with a cross-flow of 2.5-3 m/s. Target spot ~75 um (1/e2), layer 30-60 um, powder AlSi10Mg and Al-Mg-Sc-Zr, some Mg- and Zn-bearing alloys.

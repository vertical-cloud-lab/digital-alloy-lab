We are designing a small research laser powder bed fusion (LPBF) machine for screening aluminium alloys (AlSi10Mg, Al-Mg-Sc-Zr, grain-refined 2xxx/6xxx/7xxx) from 50-300 g powder lots. The current design: a 500 W single-mode CW fibre laser (1070 nm); a 2-axis galvanometer scanner with an f = 160 mm f-theta lens (about 75 um spot) above a 50.8 x 8 mm fused-silica window in the lid, with a removable 3 mm fused-silica cover slide swept by a gas knife; a bare 6061-T6 aluminium chamber of 280 x 190 x 120 mm (about 6.4 L) sealed with FKM 75A O-ring face seals, vacuum-purged with a dry scroll pump to about 1 mbar, back-filled with argon 5.0 and run at +5 to +20 mbar with a recirculating sealed blower, cyclone and HEPA filter, giving about 2 m/s cross-flow through 120 x 8 mm slots across the plate; interchangeable heated build modules (25, 50 or 75 mm diameter plates, 40 mm stroke) with 24 V cartridge heaters to 200 C in a 6061 heater block, a Macor standoff thermal break, an aramid-felt plus carbon-filled-PTFE scraper seal on a cooler piston head, a carbon-filled-PTFE rim wiper on the plate, the plate held on a bayonet (a pull-stud drawbar is planned), and a magnetic Z coupling; a dose-from-above fluted-roller metering base fed by swappable 38-233 cm3 stainless wedge hoppers with 8 mm outlet slots; a high-speed-steel blade on a ball-screw recoater at about 50-150 mm/s with 30-50 um layers; a drop-down chamber door for robot access.

Please review the peer-reviewed literature (and machine-builder documentation where it is the only source), with citations and quantitative values:

For each subsystem above, which failure modes and operating problems are reported for research, open-architecture and desktop LPBF machines: powder leaking past build pistons and into the gaps around heated plates; piston seal friction, stick-slip and wear; recoater blade wear, nicks and streaking; short feed; recoater collisions with spatter or curled edges; vibration-induced powder bed defects; spatter and condensate on the window and cover slide and the resulting focal shift or power loss; cross-flow non-uniformity and recirculation in small chambers; oxygen and moisture ingress; heater and thermocouple failures; thermal drift of the optics; Z-axis positioning accuracy and layer-thickness error; electrostatic charging of powder.

4. Very small build plates (25-75 mm): reported edge effects in packing density near the cylinder wall, heat accumulation, gas-flow effects, and how much of the plate is usable for test specimens.

We are a university lab building a small research laser powder bed fusion (LPBF) machine for screening aluminium alloys (AlSi10Mg first, then 6061 and 7075 with grain refiners) from small powder batches (30-45 g per build, 20-63 um gas-atomised). To have a working machine within 3-6 weeks it is built from second-hand and stock parts: a used IPG YLR-400-SM single-mode CW fibre laser (1070 nm) taken from an EOS machine, collimated to 5 mm, a used SCANLAB hurrySCAN 25 galvo head with an RTC5 card, a 254 mm f-theta lens (76 um spot), and a fused-silica window with a cover slide 145 mm above the bed. The powder bed is a 40 mm diameter substrate on a heated aluminium plate (200 C, cartridge heaters) that slides in a 40.00 mm bore of a block heated to the same temperature (0.1 mm radial clearance, a fibreglass rope wiper at the plate edge), driven by a thin hollow rod through an O-ring gland in the floor of a 304 stainless NEMA 4X box (about 23 L of gas) that is flow-purged with argon (no vacuum) at about 20 L/min, with a 45 x 4 mm slot nozzle giving a 2.5 m/s cross-flow over the plate. Powder is spread by a moving gravity hopper (about 30 cm3, slot outlet, vibration motor) carrying a 3 mm silicone-cord blade, after Bidare et al. 2017 (Heriot-Watt). A later version moves the same deck into a 12 in. aluminium vacuum cube pump-purged to below 100 ppm O2.

Please review the literature (journal papers, theses, conference papers such as the Solid Freeform Fabrication Symposium, HardwareX, laser manufacturer application notes and open-hardware projects) and answer each question with sources and numbers:

1. Back reflection: when a single-mode fibre laser processes aluminium with a galvo scanner and an f-theta lens, the beam meets the plate at normal incidence near the field centre, and liquid and solid aluminium reflect most of 1070 nm light. What back-reflected power fractions into the delivery fibre have been measured or modelled in LPBF or laser welding of Al, Cu and other reflective metals? What damage or shutdowns of IPG YLR single-mode lasers have been reported, and what do IPG and others recommend (back-reflection-protected models, isolators, tilting the beam or the part, offsetting the scan field, non-telecentric angle)? How do research LPBF rigs handle it?

2. Build pistons in small research LPBF machines: what plate-to-bore clearances, wiper or seal materials (felt, PTFE, graphite, ceramic or glass-fibre rope, bristles) and heating arrangements were used, especially with a heated plate at 150-200 C; what powder leakage, jamming, plate tilt or wear problems were reported, and how were they solved? Any precedent for a heated plate sliding in a bore heated to the same temperature?

3. Moving gravity hoppers with a compliant blade (rubber, silicone, carbon fibre brush) for fine aluminium powders: what outlet slot sizes, vibration and dosing control worked for 20-63 um AlSi10Mg; what layer quality problems (streaks, short feed, powder drag of thin walls) were reported, and what powder dose factor (dose volume over layer volume) did small machines need?

4. Preheating small plates: in small builds on a 40 mm plate at 150-200 C with a few hundred watts of laser power, how much does heat accumulate from layer to layer, how was the plate temperature measured and controlled, and what effect did 200 C preheating have on cracking, porosity and residual stress in AlSi10Mg and in crack-prone Al alloys (6061, 7075, Al-Cu)?

5. Flow-purged (non-vacuum) small chambers: what O2 and moisture levels and purge times are reported for 10-50 L metal or polymer chambers with argon flow, where are the usual leak points (lid gaskets, cable glands, rod seals), and how were trace O2 and dew point measured (zirconia vs electrochemical cells, sampling point)? What O2 level is needed to avoid measurable property loss in AlSi10Mg coupons?

6. In-situ monitoring on a minimal research LPBF machine: which sensors gave the most information for their cost (a layer camera with raking light for spreading defects, a coaxial photodiode or pyrometer, acoustic emission for cracking in crack-prone Al alloys, thermocouples), and what pitfalls were reported (galvo mirror coatings that block visible or NIR light, synchronising sensor data with the scan card, sampling rates)?

7. Window and cover-slide fouling: in small chambers with a cross-flow during Al LPBF, how fast do windows or cover slides lose transmission from condensate and spatter, what thermal lensing or focal shift did dirty windows cause, and how often were cover slides changed?

Please end with a ranked list of the five risks in this design most likely to cost weeks, each with the cheapest check that could be run in the first week.

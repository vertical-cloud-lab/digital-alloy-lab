| PTFE | Typically 200–260, grade-dependent | **4.21 × 10⁻⁵ after 25 h; 9.99 × 10⁻⁶ after 50 h; <1.50 × 10⁻⁶ after 100 h** at 293 K for cleaned, untreated samples (pqac-00000023) | Good after extended pump-down; initially appreciable outgassing | Generally suitable if the specific wire and termination are rated ≥200°C | Cold flow/creep can compromise gland compression; relatively permeable; initial outgassing may impair O₂/dew-point performance; verify laser-spatter exposure, bend radius, connector temperature, and complete cable construction—not insulation alone. | Kapton/polyimide | Typically 200–260; some specialized constructions higher | No quantitative value established by the cited evidence; use lot-specific ASTM E595 or measured assembly data | Very good when uncoated and properly baked; adhesive-backed tape may be substantially worse | Usually preferred for fixed 200°C internal wiring if mechanically protected | Thin insulation can abrade, crack at tight bends, or be damaged by powder/recoater motion; adhesives and overcoats—not the polyimide film itself—often dominate outgassing.

### Remaining Issues (Minor — none are critical)

**1. Time value discrepancy (~3.5 days vs. ~4.2 days)** — The throughput comparison box says "~3.5 days / alloy" while the MVS breakdown totals to ~4.2 days (34 hrs at 8 hr/day). This could be intentional (e.g., 3.5 days with some parallelization vs. 4.2 sequential), but should be reconciled or a clarifying note added. A reviewer might flag this inconsistency.

**2. Phase I/II boundary prominence** — The boundary marker exists and is correctly placed, but for a DOE proposal where Phase I scope definition is critical for evaluation, a more visually prominent treatment (full-width dashed line, shaded background change, or bolder text) would strengthen the figure.

**3. Defect hierarchy stage-mapping arrows** — The "→ Stage X" annotations in the defect hierarchy panel are quite small. The concept is excellent (mapping defect types to the stage where they're screened), but the visual connection could be slightly more prominent.

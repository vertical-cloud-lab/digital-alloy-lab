## 5. Narrative Improvements

### §1 Background (~300w)
1. **Lead with urgency, not statistics.** After "100% of its manganese" import statistic, add a CONSEQUENCE sentence: "A single supply disruption could strand X tons of planned AM production." AMMTO funds solutions to threats, not interesting facts.
2. **Sharpen competitive landscape** with a single contrast sentence: "Existing platforms either stop at powder synthesis (A-Lab) or optimize process parameters without closing the composition loop (ORNL Peregrine) — FLAIME is the first to unify composition discovery with full manufacturing qualification." More forceful than a list, fewer words.
3. **Add a "why now" signal** (~15w): "Recent advances in cost-aware BO (Alvi et al. 2026) and multi-stage function networks (Astudillo & Frazier 2021) make autonomous powder-to-property optimization computationally tractable for the first time."
4. **Strengthen D4 PI credibility** with relevance framing: "The PI led the structural materials section of the Chemical Reviews SDL review — the most comprehensive assessment of SDLs for the exact material class targeted by FLAIME."

### §2 Objectives (~250w)
1. **Front-load O1-O5 as a numbered list** (~70-80w) immediately after the section header. Reviewers scanning a 5-page proposal look for numbered objectives; burying them in prose reduces impact.
2. **Add an integrating sentence** after the list: "Collectively, these objectives demonstrate that BOFN-guided autonomous experimentation can simultaneously accelerate alloy discovery AND reduce critical mineral dependence — the dual mandate of Topic 3B."
3. **Phase II vision:** Choose ONE vivid example rather than three vague ones. "Phase II will extend FLAIME to AMMTO priority alloys (e.g., 316H for nuclear structural applications)" is more memorable than mentioning nuclear + quantum + other.
4. **Add a scope statement** (~15w): "Phase I focuses exclusively on Al alloys processed by LPBF, with Cu-Cr-Zr as a stretch demonstration." Prevents reviewer concern about overreach.

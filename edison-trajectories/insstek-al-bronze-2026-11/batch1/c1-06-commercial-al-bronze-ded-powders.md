# Commercial aluminum-bronze powder baselines for blown-powder DED

**Bottom line.** The strongest independently documented *commercial powder* is gas-atomized **Oerlikon Metco 51NS, CuAl9.5Fe**. The strongest *machine-matched purchasing lead* is InssTek’s qualified **“Al-Bronze / UNS C95300”**, but its stated powder mean and DMT tensile strengths come only from the database summary supplied with this question. Neither the retrieved literature nor that summary establishes a currently purchasable, traceable **≥2 kg lot in a specified 45–150, 50–150, or 53–150 µm cut**. No retrieved study demonstrates that such a powder yields an *as-deposited*, corrosion-qualified repair on both cast C95800 and wrought Cu–Ni that matches wrought C71500 in seawater. (uhlmann2024acomparativeevaluation pages 2-4, freisse2015drymetalforming pages 1-3, shahriari2023corrosionperformanceof pages 14-14)

## Size comparison and purchasing decision

Uhlmann’s directly measured **commercial 51NS Xₐ,50 is 95 µm**; ultrasonic NAB **Xₐ,50 is 98 µm**. Their upper percentiles are **135 versus 174 µm**: a 150-µm upper sieve limit therefore requires particular scrutiny for the ultrasonic lot. The supplied InssTek figure is an **approximately 83.8-µm *average***, not a measured **d50**; even if a subsequently measured lot had d50 ≈84 µm, that median would be **14 µm below** the ultrasonic NAB’s 98 µm and would say nothing by itself about oversize powder, fines or feeder behavior. The powders also differ substantially in composition, so size is not a controlled alloy comparison. (uhlmann2024acomparativeevaluation pages 6-7, uhlmann2024acomparativeevaluation media 2d597ecc, uhlmann2024acomparativeevaluation pages 7-10)

For a **≥2-kg commercial baseline**, pursue **InssTek C95300 first as the machine-qualified process-control lot**, conditional on a written ≥2-kg quotation, a 45–150 µm-compatible sieve specification and a lot certificate; pursue **Metco 51NS second as the independently characterized, lower-Al commercial chemistry reference**, conditional on confirmation of its exact product designation and supplier-sieved cut. If neither condition is documented, **no ≥2-kg DED-cut baseline has been verified**. Ask for measured lot D10/D50/D90 and oversize fraction, composition including Ni/Fe/Al, oxygen, image-based shape, **Hall** flow, apparent density and packaging; do not substitute Uhlmann’s **Carney** values for Hall data. A same-lot feeder/deposit trial on both proposed substrate families and a matched as-deposited wrought-C71500 seawater test remain experimental tasks, not reported outcomes. (uhlmann2024acomparativeevaluation pages 2-4, uhlmann2024acomparativeevaluation pages 10-12, freisse2015drymetalforming pages 1-3, shahriari2023corrosionperformanceof pages 14-14)

## Top 5 things the PI should know

1. **Do not claim a purchased ≥2-kg NAB baseline yet.** InssTek C95300 is the most relevant machine-qualified lead, but quantity, DED cut, lot quality and as-deposited corrosion remain unverified; it contains **no Ni** and may exceed **9.5% Al**.
2. **Metco 51NS is the best documented commercial powder comparator**, with measured **64/95/135 µm** area-equivalent percentiles, **0.013 wt% oxygen** and **10.55 s/150 g Carney flow**; **51F-NS interchangeability and an orderable DED cut are unproved**. (uhlmann2024acomparativeevaluation pages 2-4, uhlmann2024acomparativeevaluation pages 6-7, uhlmann2024acomparativeevaluation pages 10-12)
3. **A 45–125 µm CuAl10Fe1 powder has actually been laser-fed onto NAB-family bronze**, but the published build used **2.5 kW**, not the proposed 500-W ceiling, and did not measure seawater corrosion. (freisse2015drymetalforming pages 1-3, freisse2015drymetalforming pages 3-6)
4. **The 83.8-µm InssTek average is not d50.** Uhlmann’s ultrasonic NAB is **48/98/174 µm**, substantially broader at the coarse end; its measured **6.76% Fe and 9.83% Al** also make it a powder-processing comparator rather than an in-bounds design composition. (uhlmann2024acomparativeevaluation pages 6-7, uhlmann2024acomparativeevaluation pages 7-10)
5. **The decisive scientific gap is simultaneous qualification**, on *traceable pre-alloyed powder* and *as-deposited* blown-powder builds: density/cracking, interfaces to **cast C95800 and wrought Cu–Ni**, and matched-duration/flow **natural or specified artificial seawater** corrosion against **wrought C71500**. Wire-arc static-NaCl, cavitation and LPBF results provide mechanisms and benchmarks, **not proof of that target**. (shahriari2023corrosionperformanceof pages 14-14, cai2025comparativeanalysisof pages 2-3, huang2026densificationbehaviorand pages 2-4)

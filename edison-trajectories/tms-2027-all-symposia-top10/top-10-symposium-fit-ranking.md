# TMS 2027 — Top-10 Symposium Fits for the Characterization-Parameter-Optimization Abstract

**Task (issue #102 / PR #103, @sgbaird 2026-07-09):** sweep *all* TMS 2027 symposia — flyer scope +
every organizing committee — and rank the top 10 best homes for this abstract, hidden gems included.

**Method.** All **106** TMS 2027 CFA flyers (`TMS2027-CFA-Flyer-001.pdf … -106.pdf`) were downloaded and
text-extracted; each flyer's scope, topic bullets, sponsors, and organizing committee were parsed. A
weighted keyword-relevance pass over the full text of all 106 surfaced candidates (characterization / AI-ML
/ FAIR-data / AM / aluminum / microstructure-composition / workflow), and the top ~25 were deep-read by
scope and organizer persona. The full machine-extracted roster is in
[`all-106-symposia-index.md`](all-106-symposia-index.md).

**What we're placing.** A closed-loop, **agentic decision-support system** that recommends and iteratively
refines acquisition parameters across **SEM/EDS/EBSD/XRD/XRF** for high-fidelity **compositional &
microstructural** analysis of **AM aluminum**, grounded in (1) **RAG over standards** (ASTM E1508/E2627),
(2) **electron-beam simulation**, and (3) a **provenance-tracked / FAIR parameter-sweep dataset**;
validated against orthogonal **ICP-MS/MS**. Thesis = *"collect **less** data, more intentionally"* — the
opposite of a big-data / in-situ story.

> ⚠️ **Deadline reality (applies to every option below).** Every flyer lists **Abstract Submissions Due
> July 1, 2026.** As of 2026-07-09 that has **passed.** Confirm late/rolling submission on ProgramMaster
> before committing anywhere. Flyer **031 (Hume-Rothery) is invited-abstract-only** and cannot be
> submitted to cold.

> **Companion-abstract routing (avoid self-collision).** #100 (LPBF) already targets **Flyer 021**; #98
> (atomization) is closest to **Flyer 059 / 001**. Rankings below flag where routing #102 would double-book
> a FLAIME abstract into the same room.

## Detailed rationale

### 1 — Flyer 022 · AI/ML/Data Informatics for Materials Discovery ★★★★★  *(new top pick)*
The single closest scope match found across all 106. Topic bullets we hit almost verbatim: **"Large
language models, multimodal foundation models, and agentic AI for … research workflow assistance"**;
**"Novel approaches for AI-driven data generation, extraction, cleaning, annotation, fusion, and curation"**;
**"FAIR data principles, metadata frameworks, provenance, and interoperable infrastructure"**; **"Hybrid
approaches that combine AI/ML/DI with physics-based models"**; **"Integration of experimental,
computational, and literature-derived data."** Our RAG-over-standards + simulation-grounded + FAIR
provenance-dataset system *is* this symposium's thesis.
**Organizer signal:** **Taylor Sparks (University of Utah)** — a marquee data-materials name and a
neighbor institution; also **James Saal (Citrine)**, **Kamal Choudhary (JHU)**, **Christopher Stiles (JHU
APL)**, **Daniel Wines (NIST)**. This roster rewards exactly the "grounded, physics- and standards-anchored
AI" pitch and is wary of hype — which matches our honest, early-stage framing.
**Tailor:** lead with the agentic/RAG + FAIR-dataset system as the contribution; keep the trace-Mg / EBSD-CI
numbers as the credibility anchor showing the AI is grounded in real microanalysis, not pixels.

### 2 — Flyer 086 · MSD & Advanced Characterization, Honoring D.T. Fullwood ★★★★★  *(home venue)*
**Organized by BYU** — Oliver Johnson, Eric Homer, Michael Miles — plus Niezgoda (OSU), Knezevic (UNH),
Kacher (GT), Ruggles (Sandia). Scope centers **EBSD/HR-EBSD to quantify and model** microstructure and
deformation, and **microstructure-sensitive design**. Our EBSD indexing/phase-model/beam-current study and
microstructural-fidelity thesis land squarely in the "Advanced Characterization Techniques" bullet, and the
authors are BYU (Baird's group; Fullwood is BYU faculty). Highest **social/institutional capital** of any
option.
**Tailor:** foreground the EBSD (and broader microstructure) side and how principled acquisition improves
indexing fidelity; treat the AI/RAG layer as the enabling method, not the headline. A tribute symposium
wants work in the honoree's lineage — EBSD-for-microstructure is exactly that.

### 3 — Flyer 024 · AI-ICME ★★★★★
Bullet-level matches: **"AI-enabled microstructure characterization and structure-property inference from
multimodal data (images, spectra, processing history)"**; **"Active learning, Bayesian optimization, and
autonomous/closed-loop experimentation … for … processing optimization"**; **"Data curation, standards,
provenance/traceability, and reproducible … MLOps"**; **"foundation models including LLMs for knowledge
extraction, workflow assistance, and decision support."** Adds an explicit **certification/adoption**
framing that suits a standards-grounded metrology tool. Deep organizer bench (LLNL/PNNL/LANL/GE).
**Tailor:** cast the closed loop as a process→structure→property decision-support node inside ICME, with
the parameter-fidelity dataset as the reusable ICME data asset.

### 4 — Flyer 021 · AI-Enabled Materials Processing ★★★★☆  *(collision risk)*
Scope explicitly wants **"closed-loop experimental workflows that integrate synthesis, processing,
characterization, testing, and iterative model-guided refinement"** and **Bayesian/adaptive
experimentation** — our loop, essentially. **But #100 (LPBF) is already routed here**, and two FLAIME
abstracts in one room dilutes both. Rank this a strong *scope* fit that is **strategically blocked** unless
#100 relocates (e.g., to 003/005) or the powder→print→characterize trio deliberately co-locates.

### 5 — Flyer 012 · Rapid Acquisition & Processing of Large Datasets ★★★☆☆  *(incumbent)*
Already the abstract's listed target and already reviewed in depth (see
`../tms-2027-compositional-analysis-abstract-review/`). Real overlaps: **FAIR curation of large
characterization metadata**, **accelerated feature extraction from EBSD/imaging/diffraction via CV/ML**,
and McDannald's active-learning acquisition. But the room's center of mass is **in-situ / high-throughput /
synchrotron / large-dataset**, the near-inverse of "collect less, more intentionally." Keep as a viable
home **only with the hard active-acquisition + FAIR-dataset reframe**; otherwise 022/024 dominate it.

### 6 — Flyer 044 · Aluminum Alloys: Development & Fabrication ★★★☆☆  *(native material home)*
The natural **aluminum** room (TMS Aluminum Committee; Novelis/Kaiser/OSU organizers). Scope lists
**Microstructure Evolution and Characterization**, **Defect Detection and Control**, **Process Control and
Measurements** — our content qualifies. Trade-off: it's an **industrial physical-metallurgy** audience less
tuned to agentic-AI novelty, and **an oral talk requires a Light Metals proceedings manuscript** (real extra
work). Best when the goal is credibility with the Al community rather than the AI/data community.

### 7 — Flyer 023 · Algorithms Development in MSE ★★★☆☆  *(hidden gem)*
Easy to overlook behind the flashier AI symposia, but the scope literally invites **"Development of agentic
workflows for planning, decision-making, and orchestration of complex materials [workflows]"**, plus
**"algorithm development for fusing and evaluating the quality of multimodal data"** and **FAIRization**.
That is the *systems/orchestration* backbone of our tool. Sandia/ORNL/UC-Davis organizers reward a
well-posed method + V&V. Pitch the architecture (RAG + simulation + dataset as a decision algorithm), not
just the aluminum results.

### 8 — Flyer 003 · AM Modeling, Simulation, and AI ★★★☆☆
A long-established, well-attended **AM + AI/ML** venue; bullet 4 (*"AM process monitoring and defect
quantification for model verification and validation"*) gives our metrology a toehold, and ML/AI/UQ-for-AM
is explicitly in scope. Weaker because the room skews toward **predictive process/microstructure modeling**
rather than ex-situ compositional metrology — our characterization angle would be a minority flavor.

### 9 — Flyer 005 · Designing Complex Microstructures Through AM ★★★☆☆
Names **"Compositional and microstructural control in metal AM"** and **"Computational, experimental, and
data-driven approaches for computer-aided AM"**, and lists characterization among the expertise it convenes.
The emphasis is on **engineering** site-specific microstructures rather than **faithfully measuring**
composition/microstructure — so our fidelity-of-measurement thesis is adjacent, not central.

### 10 — Flyer 075 · Powder Materials Processing & Fundamental Understanding ★★☆☆☆
Rounds out the list on the **powder-characterization** angle (our feedstock samples): scope includes
**"Advanced experimental techniques for powder characterization"** and **"Machine learning and data-driven
discovery in powder materials science."** Powder-mechanistic focus makes it a stretch for a full
multi-modal metrology talk; consider **059 (Atomization & PM, Honoring Iver Anderson)** instead if the
powder-analysis framing is wanted (bullet: *"Advanced powder material analysis and characterization"*) —
though #98 is the more natural occupant of 059.

## Hidden gems (outside the 10, worth a deliberate look)

- **Flyer 105 · Accelerating Innovation in Materials & Manufacturing** *(Special Topics)* — a
  **supply-chain-resilience + AI** program (MIT/JPL organizers) with a **midday pitch competition for
  students, postdocs, and early-career researchers.** A near-perfect stage for the **FLAIME
  supply-chain-resilient-aluminum + AI** narrative and for **Ronnie Guymon** to get visibility. Caveat:
  the format is **invited talks + moderated panels + a pitch competition**, not a standard contributed-oral
  slot — treat it as a *complementary* high-visibility venue, not the technical home.
- **Flyer 025 · Avoiding Plot Holes: Telling Compelling Stories (with Data)** *(Data-Driven)* — a craft
  symposium on **scientific communication, data visualization, and responsible LLM use**, co-sponsored by
  the **Advanced Characterization Committee**. Off the technical mainline, but a memorable home for a talk
  built around the **provenance-tracked dataset, reproducibility, and "when to trust the model"** story.
- **Flyer 010 · 4D STEM for Quantitative Materials Characterization** *(Advanced Char)* — has an explicit
  **"validation and metrology … accuracy, precision, uncertainty quantification, and standards"** +
  **reproducibility** bullet that resonates with our fidelity thesis. Held back only by **technique
  mismatch** (we don't run 4D-STEM).
- **Flyer 008 · High-Resolution Coherent Imaging VIII** *(Advanced Char)* — **BYU's Richard Sandberg**
  co-organizes, but the content (CDI/ptychography/synchrotron) is far from our SEM/EDS/EBSD suite; the BYU
  tie doesn't overcome the low content fit.

## Recommendation

Three genuine homes, pick by which audience you want:

1. **If the star is the AI/data system → Flyer 022** (AI/ML/Data Informatics). Best pure-scope match in the
   whole program; Utah-adjacent, hype-skeptical roster that rewards our grounded framing.
2. **If the star is the microstructure/EBSD science + the BYU network → Flyer 086** (Fullwood MSD). Home
   venue, EBSD-core, maximal social capital — lead with the characterization content.
3. **Strong third / ICME framing → Flyer 024** (AI-ICME).

**Avoid** routing here into **021** (owned by #100) unless the trio deliberately co-locates. **012** remains
viable but only with the hard reframe, and 022/024 now out-fit it.

**Verdict: both 022 and 024 beat both incumbents for this abstract.** The panel is explicit — this is an
AI/data contribution wearing a characterization application, so it belongs in an AI/data-native room where
the agentic architecture, RAG-over-standards, and FAIR provenance schema *are* the contribution rather than
backdrop.

**Single best home = Flyer 022.** Panel-suggested reframe (preserved verbatim in the result file):
- **Retitle to lead with the data-informatics contribution**, e.g. *"An Agentic, Standards-Grounded
  Decision-Support System for FAIR Characterization-Parameter Optimization: Architecture,
  Retrieval-Augmented Reasoning, and Validation on AM Aluminum Alloys."*
- **Open on the data-centric problem**: characterization metadata is the least standardized/least-FAIR link
  in the materials-data pipeline (machine-readable SOPs, provenance schemas).
- **Present the three-source-of-truth architecture** (RAG over ASTM E1508/E2627, interaction-volume
  simulation as physics-informed inductive bias, provenance-tracked sweeps) as the core contribution.
- **Demote AlSi10Mg/eZAF-Mg/EBSD-CI to validation vignettes**, not the headline.
- **Close on the FAIR dataset as a community resource** (multimodal data curation/extraction bullet).

For **024** the one lever is a single sentence tying optimized characterization to downstream ICME utility
(e.g., corrected EBSD phase models → tighter ODF uncertainty → tighter anisotropic-yield bounds) + a
certification/traceability nod for Pilania (GE Aerospace).

## Residual notes (advisory only — not addressable from the draft alone)

These were honestly flagged as v2 out-of-scope items and remain so;
the framework cannot resolve them and a human PO sanity-check is the
right next step.

1. **Funding-opportunity-specific length limits.** §I.D.1 defers
   "specific guidance on length limitations" to the relevant DCL or
   program solicitation, not PAPPG. The 2-page draft is well under
   any plausible cap, but the framework can't confirm the exact
   limit for an unsolicited concept outline to ENG/CMMI Advanced
   Manufacturing.
2. **Currency of the cognizant unit.** The framework can't tell
   whether `ENG/CMMI Advanced Manufacturing` is *the* current
   cognizant unit for VCL-style work or whether a recent reorg
   moved it; that signal lives outside PAPPG. The v3 expansion to
   three candidate units already mitigates this — even if Advanced
   Manufacturing is no longer the right primary home, the listed
   secondaries give the recipient PO a clean redirect path.

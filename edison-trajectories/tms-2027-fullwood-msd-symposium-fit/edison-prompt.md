THE ABSTRACT UNDER REVIEW (BYU Vertical Cloud Lab, FLAIME project):
Title: "Multi-Modal Characterization-Parameter Optimization for Compositional & Microstructural Analysis of AM Alloys." We are building a closed-loop, agentic decision-support system that, given a sample (AlSi10Mg and lean, supply-chain-resilient aluminum alloys, as gas-/ultrasonically-atomized powder and LPBF prints) and an analysis goal, recommends and iteratively refines acquisition/processing parameters across SEM, EDS, EBSD, XRD, and XRF. Three grounding pillars: (1) retrieval-augmented generation over characterization standards/texts (ASTM E1508 for EDS, E2627 for EBSD, ICP standards, Goldstein microanalysis); (2) physics-based electron-beam / interaction-volume (Monte Carlo, φ(ρz)) simulation to choose settings a priori; (3) an accumulating, provenance-tracked dataset of parameter sweeps validated against orthogonal ICP-MS/MS, functioning as in-house "standards." Concrete motivating results: standardless eZAF over-reports trace Mg in AlSi10Mg several-fold (nominal 0.2–0.45 wt% read as ~1.4–1.6 wt%) due to the Mg Kα / Al Kα overlap and soft-X-ray background modeling; EBSD confidence-index yield on LPBF Al–Si rose from ~40% to ~77% (CI>0.1) when beam current went 1.6→6.4 nA with a custom AlSi10Mg + diamond-cubic-Si phase model. Deliverable: a labeled, provenance-tracked characterization-parameter dataset to make expert instrument setup reproducible and transferable for metal-AM compositional/microstructural analysis.

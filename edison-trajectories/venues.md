# Venues

*Edison Scientific LITERATURE_HIGH query*

Question: What are the top venues (journals and conferences) for publishing research on AI-accelerated alloy development, autonomous materials discovery, laser powder bed fusion additive manufacturing, and high-throughput materials characterization?

The research program is at BYU (Brigham Young University) in a new lab called the Vertical Cloud Lab (VCL) focused on digital alloy development using:
- AI/ML-accelerated alloy composition screening and design
- Autonomous powder production via ultrasonic atomization (Amazemet rePOWDER)
- Laser powder bed fusion (LPBF) additive manufacturing for rapid alloy validation
- High-throughput characterization (SEM/EDS/EBSD, PIP indentation plastometry)
- Closed-loop integration connecting computational prediction to experimental validation

The PI is an early-career assistant professor. The alloy systems of interest include aluminum alloys, high-entropy alloys, and compositionally complex alloys for structural applications. The lab emphasizes autonomous experimentation, materials informatics, and accelerated materials discovery.

Rank and categorize by: (1) high-impact journals for computational/AI materials research, (2) high-impact journals for experimental alloy development, (3) journals bridging computation and experiment, (4) key conferences for presenting this work, (5) workshop venues for building community. Include impact factors where available and note which venues are most valued by NSF and DoD program managers.

# Top Venues for AI-Accelerated Alloy Development, Autonomous Materials Discovery, LPBF Additive Manufacturing, and High-Throughput Characterization

## Overview

This report identifies and ranks the most impactful publication venues for the Vertical Cloud Lab (VCL) at BYU, a research program integrating AI/ML-accelerated alloy screening, autonomous powder production, laser powder bed fusion (LPBF) additive manufacturing, and high-throughput characterization in a closed-loop digital alloy development pipeline. The analysis draws on bibliometric evidence from recent review articles and primary research papers spanning computational materials science, experimental alloy development, and autonomous experimentation (wang2023alphamatamaterial pages 16-18, bukkapatnam2023autonomousmaterialsdiscovery pages 16-17, yuan2024applicationsofmachine pages 16-17, bukkapatnam2023autonomousmaterialsdiscovery pages 20-20).

## Notes on NSF and DoD Program Manager Preferences

**NSF (DMREF, OAC, CMMI):** NSF program managers highly value publications in *Nature* family journals (Nature Communications, npj Computational Materials, Nature Computational Science), *Science/Science Advances*, and *Acta Materialia* (miret2026ai4maticlr2026iclr2026 pages 9-11, yuan2024applicationsofmachine pages 16-17). Attendance and presentations at *MRS meetings* and *TMS meetings* demonstrate community engagement. Alignment with the Materials Genome Initiative through DMREF-relevant venues (IMMI, MRS Bulletin) is essential for CAREER and DMREF proposals (miret2026ai4maticlr2026iclr2026 pages 9-11, bukkapatnam2023autonomousmaterialsdiscovery pages 17-18).

**DoD (ONR, AFOSR, DARPA):** DoD program managers value applied results in *JOM*, *IMMI*, *Materials Science and Engineering A*, and *Additive Manufacturing* (sau2022highthroughputnanoindentationmapping pages 16-16, bukkapatnam2023autonomousmaterialsdiscovery pages 17-18). The *TMS Annual Meeting* is the primary conference where DoD materials program managers engage with the academic community. Publications demonstrating rapid qualification of new alloys for structural applications—particularly through LPBF and high-throughput mechanical testing—align with DoD's accelerated materials insertion priorities. *Applied Physics Reviews* is valued for comprehensive reviews relevant to defense applications (bukkapatnam2023autonomousmaterialsdiscovery pages 17-18).

**For an early-career assistant professor**, the most strategic approach is to maintain a publication mix across tiers: aim for 1–2 flagship papers in Nature Communications/Science Advances to anchor tenure and CAREER cases, build a steady output in specialty journals (npj Computational Materials, Additive Manufacturing, Acta Materialia, Digital Discovery) for community recognition, and use JOM/IMMI/Met Trans A for broader translation and DoD engagement. Consistent presence at TMS, MRS, and AI4Mat workshops will maximize visibility to both funding agencies and the growing autonomous materials discovery community (bukkapatnam2023autonomousmaterialsdiscovery pages 16-17, miret2024perspectiveonai pages 4-5, miret2024perspectiveonai pages 1-2).

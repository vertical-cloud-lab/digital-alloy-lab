## PART A: Title Analysis for Topic 11C Fit

### A.1 Overall Alignment Score: 3/10

The current title was purpose-built for Topic 12F (AMMTO) and reads as an applied manufacturing/materials development proposal. Topic 11C (BES) explicitly calls for "AI-enabled identification of phenomena or correlations in high-throughput data that are not consistent with or otherwise predicted by current scientific models and generation of a plausible new explanation as a testable hypothesis in a truly closed-loop process for advancing science." The DOE AI for Science report similarly emphasizes that BES priorities center on "closed-loop materials property prediction, synthesis, and characterization" and "autonomous-smart experiments" that "generate scientific data in a rational way, formulating new physical models and theoretical insights" (stevens2020aiforscience pages 28-29). The current title contains none of these signals—no mention of closed-loop science, hypotheses, correlations, understanding, or phenomena.

**Detailed keyword analysis:**

- **"Autonomous"** — Partially aligned. Topic 11C values autonomy in the sense of AI-driven autonomous laboratories that integrate hypothesis generation, experimental execution, and scientific interpretation in closed-loop cycles (tom2024selfdrivinglaboratoriesfor pages 2-3, lee2026towardselfdrivinglaboratory pages 1-2). However, "autonomous" alone is ambiguous; it could mean automated manufacturing (an AMMTO reading) or an SDL-style closed-loop science platform (a BES reading). Without modifiers like "closed-loop," "self-driving laboratory," or "hypothesis-generating," a BES PM will default to the manufacturing interpretation given the rest of the title.

- **"Powder-to-Part"** — Misaligned. This phrase is manufacturing pipeline branding. It frames the work as a production flow (feedstock → component) rather than a scientific inquiry. BES reviewers will read this as an engineering deliverable, not a scientific investigation. The SDL literature frames analogous platforms around "discovery cycles" and "iterative hypothesis formulation" rather than production-chain metaphors (delgadolicona2023researchaccelerationin pages 2-2, abolhasani2023theriseof pages 2-3).

- **"Discovery and Qualification"** — Mixed. "Discovery" is acceptable for 11C but insufficiently specific—BES wants discovery of *what*? New phenomena? New correlations? Testable hypotheses? "Qualification" is strongly AMMTO-coded; it signals certification/validation for deployment, which is antithetical to BES basic science framing. The DOE AI workshops distinguish "AI for scientific understanding" (using phrases like "explainable, interpretable and trusted inference" and "physics-constrained AI") from optimization/engineering language (carter2023advancedresearchdirections pages 48-49).

- **"3D-printed"** — Narrowing. While additive manufacturing is a legitimate BES topic, leading with "3D-printed" as a modifier for "Structural Alloys" makes the work sound like process optimization rather than fundamental science. BES reviewers would prefer seeing the alloy system as a scientific platform, not a manufacturing application.

- **"Structural Alloys"** — Application-specific. This signals end-use performance rather than fundamental process–structure–property understanding. BES reviewers will ask: "What new science will emerge?"

- **"Predictable Functionality"** — Directly echoes Topic 12F language ("Designing Materials with Predictable Functionality") and will be immediately recognized by reviewers as an AMMTO keyword. It carries zero BES signal and actively suggests the wrong topic.

### A.3 Missing Signals for BES/11C

A BES program manager overseeing Topic 11C would look for the following signals, none of which appear in the current title:

1. **"Closed-loop"** — The single most important term for 11C. The RFA text explicitly calls for "a truly closed-loop process for advancing science." DOE BES has emphasized "closed-loop materials property prediction, synthesis, and characterization" as a high-priority goal (stevens2020aiforscience pages 28-29).

2. **"Hypothesis generation" or "testable hypotheses"** — The RFA asks for "generation of a plausible new explanation as a testable hypothesis." Closed-loop platforms that produce genuine scientific understanding must move beyond correlation-finding to generate mechanistic, testable predictions (musslick2024closedloopscientificdiscovery pages 1-2, zenil2026thefutureof pages 1-5).

3. **"Scientific understanding," "correlations," or "phenomena"** — The RFA calls for "identification of phenomena or correlations in high-throughput data that are not consistent with or otherwise predicted by current scientific models." This is the core BES value proposition: understanding, not optimization (zenil2026thefutureof pages 5-10).

4. **"Self-driving laboratory" or "autonomous laboratory"** — Topic 11 overview is titled "Achieving AI-Driven Autonomous Laboratories." The SDL literature defines these platforms by closed-loop integration of automation, AI decision-making, and orchestration (tom2024selfdrivinglaboratoriesfor pages 2-3, lee2026towardselfdrivinglaboratory pages 1-2).

5. **"Process–structure–property"** — This phrase bridges materials science and fundamental science and is BES-native terminology for understanding causal chains in materials, as distinct from manufacturing qualification.

### A.4 RFA Language That Should Appear in the Title

From the 11C RFA text, the following phrases or their derivatives should inform the title:
- "closed-loop process for advancing science"

## PART E: Strategic Recommendation

### Decision: Rewrite the title for Topic 11C.

The current title is an AMMTO title. It was correctly crafted for Topic 12F but is fundamentally misaligned with Topic 11C. Minor modifications (e.g., adding "closed-loop" while keeping "Qualification" and "Predictable Functionality") will not resolve the core issue: the title communicates manufacturing outcomes, not scientific understanding. Given the prior mock review scores favoring 11C (5.8/10 with potential to reach 25–35%) over 12F (4/10 at 3–6%), the strategic choice is clear.

### Recommended Title:

**"FLAIME: Closed-Loop Autonomous Discovery of Testable Process–Structure–Property Hypotheses in Structural Alloys"**

(Title #4 from the recommendations table; 11C Fit: 10/10, Overall Quality: 10/10)

### Justification:

1. **"Closed-Loop"** directly mirrors the RFA's "truly closed-loop process for advancing science" and is the single most important signal for 11C. DOE BES has designated closed-loop autonomous experimentation as a high-priority goal (stevens2020aiforscience pages 28-29).

2. **"Autonomous Discovery"** signals an SDL-type platform without the manufacturing connotation of "Autonomous Powder-to-Part." The SDL literature defines these as platforms integrating automated experiments with data-driven decision-making (tom2024selfdrivinglaboratoriesfor pages 2-3, abolhasani2023theriseof pages 2-3).

3. **"Testable ... Hypotheses"** directly addresses the RFA's requirement for "generation of a plausible new explanation as a testable hypothesis." This distinguishes the proposal from mere optimization by committing to the production of scientific knowledge (musslick2024closedloopscientificdiscovery pages 1-2, zenil2026thefutureof pages 1-5).

4. **"Process–Structure–Property"** is BES-native materials science language that frames alloy investigation as fundamental science rather than engineering. It signals understanding of causal chains, not just performance targets.

5. **"Structural Alloys"** is retained to preserve the domain specificity the PI needs, and it is BES-acceptable as a material system (analogous to how thin-film semiconductors, quantum materials, or catalysts appear in BES proposals).

6. **FLAIME acronym** is preserved, maintaining the PI's branding. The full expansion "Fast Learning AI for Materials Experimentation" is broad enough for BES.

7. **Powder-to-Part** is sacrificed in the title but can and should be prominently featured in the abstract, project narrative, and milestone descriptions. The title's job is to get through the initial PM sorting and first-impression review; the abstract and narrative carry the technical detail. DOE proposal conventions show that successful titles prioritize topic-aligned language over process-chain branding (diwan2024approach pages 1-9, diwan2024approach pages 58-61).

### If the PI insists on retaining "Powder-to-Part":

The next-best option is Title #3:

**"FLAIME: Autonomous Closed-Loop Powder-to-Part Science for Discovering Emergent Alloy Correlations and Mechanisms"**

(11C Fit: 9/10, Overall Quality: 9/10)

This preserves the branding while embedding it within BES-coded language ("Closed-Loop ... Science," "Emergent ... Correlations and Mechanisms"). The word "Science" after "Powder-to-Part" reframes the manufacturing chain as a scientific investigation platform.

### Additional Strategic Notes:

- The BOFN (Bayesian Optimization of Function Networks) methodology is a genuine algorithmic innovation that models multi-step experimental workflows as directed acyclic graphs of expensive functions with independent GP surrogates per node (buathong2023bayesianoptimizationof pages 1-2, astudillo2021bayesianoptimizationof pages 1-2). This should be prominently featured in the abstract and project description as the AI methods contribution. While it does not need to appear in the title, the title should create space for the abstract to introduce it as the mechanism by which closed-loop hypothesis generation is achieved.

- The DOE AI workshops emphasize that next-generation autonomous platforms must move beyond "mere throughput optimization to integrated, discovery-oriented AI systems" (carter2023advancedresearchdirections pages 65-67). The recommended title positions the proposal squarely within this vision.

- SDL 2.0 frameworks characterize advanced autonomous laboratories as not merely optimizing but generating hypotheses and converting data into scientific knowledge, measured along axes of experiments per unit time, information gained per experiment, and efficiency of converting data into scientific understanding (lee2026towardselfdrivinglaboratory pages 1-2). The recommended title aligns with all three axes.

- Finally, the distinction between correlation and understanding is critical for BES framing: "correlations are observed associations in data, whereas understanding requires mechanistic, generalizable models that generate testable hypotheses and are interrogated through targeted experimentation" (musslick2024closedloopscientificdiscovery pages 1-2, zenil2026thefutureof pages 5-10). The proposal narrative should make this distinction explicit and position BOFN as the mechanism that enables the transition from correlation to understanding within the closed-loop alloy discovery platform.

# Image Gallery: Real Commercial Al AM Alloy Parts

Curated images of confirmed industrial deployments listed in
[`downstream_applications.md`](./downstream_applications.md), suitable for
embedding into the Phase II DAG figure in PR
[#58](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab-private/pull/58).
None of these images are CC-licensed; each must be credited and (for any non-fair-use
embedding) cleared with the source. Links go to the original publishing page, not
to a hot-linked CDN, so the original caption/credit is visible.

## AlSi10Mg

### NASA MSFC LPBF rocket-engine injector
- **Image page (NASA):** <https://www.nasa.gov/image-article/rocket-engine-injector-manufactured-with-3-d-printing-machine/>
- **Credit:** NASA / MSFC (NASA still images are generally free for non-commercial
  use; see NASA media usage guidelines).
- **Note:** Headline NASA AM-injector imagery; widely cited as the canonical
  "first 3D-printed rocket engine part" photo. The injector body alloy is most
  commonly Inconel 625; the AlSi10Mg cryogenic injector work is documented in
  the same MSFC program and shows similar form-factor parts.

### Mahle / Porsche / Trumpf 3D-printed AlSi10Mg piston (911 GT2 RS)
- **Image page (Mahle MPULSE):** <https://workshop-heroes.mahle.com/en/article/3d-printed-pistons>
- **Image page (Porsche Newsroom):** <https://newsroom.porsche.com/en_US/technology/porsche-cooperation-mahle-trumpf-pistons-3d-printer-power-efficiency-911-gt2-rs-21481.html>
- **Credit:** Mahle GmbH / Dr. Ing. h.c. F. Porsche AG.
- **Note:** Hi-res studio photos of the finished piston with the lattice
  internal-cooling structure visible. This is the closest publicly available
  imagery to the Mahle Powertrain F1 piston work referenced in
  `downstream_applications.md` (the F1 pistons themselves are not photographed
  publicly).

### Bright Laser Technologies (BLT) AlSi10Mg deployer frame, Dalian-1 Lianli CubeSat
- **Image page (BLT news):** <https://www.xa-blt.com/en/news/developed-with-the-assistance-of-blt-the-remote-sensing-satellite-was-successfully-launched-with-the-tianzhou-6-cargo-ship/>
- **Trade-press write-up with same imagery (Metal AM):** <https://www.metal-am.com/blt-develops-deployment-structure-for-cubesat-satellite/>
- **Trade-press write-up with same imagery (Formnext / Mesago):** <https://formnext.mesago.com/frankfurt/en/industry-insights/fonmag/fonmag_articles/articles/AM-structure-for-remote-sensing-satellites.html>
- **Credit:** Bright Laser Technologies (Xi'an).

## Scalmalloy® (Al-Mg-Sc-Zr)

### Premium AEROTEC / APWorks Scalmalloy bracket (Airbus structural part family)
- **Image page (Airbus newsroom — Premium AEROTEC bracket production):** <https://www.airbus.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2017-09-first-titanium-3d-printed-part-installed-into-serial-production>
- **Direct image (Airbus CDN, P90296174):** <https://www.airbus.com/sites/g/files/jlcbta136/files/thumbnails/image/P90296174-highRes-production-of-the-fi.jpg>
- **Credit:** Premium AEROTEC / Airbus / APWorks.
- **Note:** The widely cited generative-design lattice bracket image. Often
  reused in trade press as the canonical "Scalmalloy structural bracket" photo.

### APWorks Light Rider (first 3D-printed Scalmalloy motorcycle frame)
- **Press release (Airbus newsroom):** <https://www.airbus.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2016-05-airbus-apworks-launches-the-light-rider-the-worlds-first-3d-printed>
- **Trade-press write-up with same imagery:** <https://additivemanufacturing.com/2016/05/27/airbus-apworks-launches-the-light-rider-the-worlds-first-3d-printed-motorcycle/>
- **Credit:** Airbus APWorks GmbH.
- **Note:** Strong visual demonstrator of Scalmalloy's strength-to-weight in a
  recognizable consumer-oriented part.

### Thales Alenia Space Scalmalloy / AlSi7Mg antenna support, Koreasat-5A / -7
- **Press release (Thales):** <https://www.thalesgroup.com/en/news-centre/press-releases/thales-alenia-space-takes-3d-printing-series-production>
- **Trade-press write-up (TCT Magazine):** <https://www.tctmagazine.com/thales-alenia-space-poly-shape-largest-3d-print/>
- **Trade-press write-up with photo credit "Photo via Thales Alenia Space" (3D Printing Industry):** <https://3dprintingindustry.com/news/thales-alenia-space-expands-exploration-larger-3d-printed-parts-113574/>
- **Credit:** Thales Alenia Space / Poly-Shape.
- **Note:** This is the Koreasat antenna support that consolidated 9 parts → 1
  with 22% mass savings — the canonical case study.

### JUICE mission Scalmalloy camera bracket
- **Best public-photo entry point (3dpbm):** <https://www.3dprintingmedia.network/juice-3d-printed-parts-jupiter-esa/>
- **ESA JUICE image gallery (search "Scalmalloy" / "bracket"):** <https://www.cosmos.esa.int/web/juice/pictures>
- **Credit:** ESA / Airbus Defence & Space (and partners as named in caption).
- **Note:** Web search did not surface a single permanent ESA URL for the
  bracket itself; the 3dpbm article is the most reliable single source with the
  part visible.

## AlSi40

### ESA / Fraunhofer "Printed Athermal Mirror" (PAM) AlSi40 cryogenic optic
- **Primary paper (SPIE, ICSO 2018):** <https://www.spiedigitallibrary.org/conference-proceedings-of-spie/11180/1118015/Additive-manufacturing-of-an-AlSi40-mirror-coated-with-electroless-nickel/10.1117/12.2535960.full>
- **DOI:** [10.1117/12.2535960](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2535960)
- **Open-access mirror (Academia.edu PDF):** <https://www.academia.edu/55206552/>
- **Follow-up paper (ICSO 2022, Fraunhofer Publica):** <https://publica.fraunhofer.de/entities/publication/bc3948e2-7e1c-4271-9fac-25e153acad88/details>
- **Credit:** Fraunhofer IOF / Eberle, Reutlinger et al.
- **Note:** Both papers contain photographs of the printed mirror substrate
  with the NiP coating; figures inside the SPIE PDF are the highest-resolution
  publicly available imagery of an AlSi40 deployed optical part.

## A20X / Aeromet AM205

- **Best entry points (no good single product photo found):**
  - A20X official site: <https://www.a20x.com>
  - Aeromet International news: <https://www.aeromet.co.uk/news/>
  - Magellan Aerospace licensing announcement (with related part imagery): <https://magellan.aero/press-release/magellan-becomes-aeromet-a20x-advanced-aluminium-licensee/>
  - University of Birmingham / AMT collaboration coverage: <https://3dprintingindustry.com/news/aluminium-materials-technologies-and-university-of-birmingham-collaborate-to-deepen-metallurgy-understanding-of-a20x-aluminum-alloy-182275/>
- **Note:** No publicly hosted image of a deployed AM205 production part was
  found via web search; A20X marketing imagery is mostly powder, coupons, and
  generic LPBF demonstrators rather than fielded components. Consistent with
  `downstream_applications.md`: A20X is at the qualification stage and no
  deployed component is named in the open literature.

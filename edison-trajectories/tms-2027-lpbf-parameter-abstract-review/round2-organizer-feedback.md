# Mock Symposium-Organizer Review — Round 2

*For the TMS 2027 abstract [`tms-2027-lpbf-process-parameter-optimization-abstract.md`](../../tms-2027-lpbf-process-parameter-optimization-abstract.md).*
*Target symposium: **AI-Enabled Materials Processing: Integrating Accelerated Experimental Workflows and Processing-Aware Machine Learning**.*

> Same caveat as [round 1](round1-organizer-feedback.md): composite reviewer aligned with the symposium's stated
> scope; **verify named organizers on ProgramMaster** before submission.

## Round-2 verdict

**Accept for an oral slot**, contingent on the minor items below. With the round-1 changes, the abstract now
leads with the AI/workflow contribution, frames transfer as a measured open question, and scopes the LLM layer
honestly. "No fatal flaws remain" for an early-stage group.

## What the reviewer liked

- **On-title fit.** "Accelerated experimental workflows" (Thermo-Calc prior → BO → escalating test ladder) +
  "processing-aware machine learning" (printability-seeded, uncertainty-aware optimization) is a near-literal
  match to the symposium name. This will not be a borderline-scope submission.
- **The transfer question is the hook.** Cross-machine *and cross-instance* transfer is under-reported (the
  same-model, instance-to-instance case especially), and pairing it with "how few experiments to re-qualify"
  gives the talk a crisp, quantifiable question.
- **Budget honesty.** Reporting "the experimental budget each requires" is exactly the kind of practical,
  reproducible framing this audience rewards, and it neutralizes the small build count.
- **Simulation-grounded prior.** Seeding the search with a Thermo-Calc AM Module printability map (rather than a
  blind sweep) is a credible physically informed prior and differentiates from pure black-box ML-LPBF papers.

## Minor items (recommended)

1. **Name one alloy for concreteness, keep the method general.** Reviewers like a named anchor. *Recommendation
   (checklist item, left to the authors):* "commercial AlSi10Mg baseline + one lean supply-chain-resilient Al
   composition," mirroring the atomization abstract, without over-committing the powder list. Kept optional in
   the checklist rather than hard-coded.
2. **Author/affiliation clarity.** LPBF printing happens on the Aconity MIDI in Taylor Sparks' lab (U of Utah);
   decide co-author vs. acknowledgement and confirm emails. Flagged prominently at the top of the abstract file.
3. **Title length.** The full working title is descriptive but long; consider a shorter presented title (e.g.,
   *"Transfer-Aware, Simulation-Seeded Optimization of LPBF Parameters, Across Powders and Machines"*) for the
   oral slot. Left as an authoring choice.
4. **LLM framing — hold the line.** Keep "pilot … augments (rather than replaces)." Do **not** upgrade to
   "autonomous" or "closed-loop LLM" before there is data; that would reintroduce the round-1 credibility risk.

## Net

Submission version stands at **147 words** (≤150). The abstract is **accept-ready** pending the author-side
confirmations (author list, optional alloy naming, and the ProgramMaster deadline / organizer verification in
the [pre-submission checklist](../../tms-2027-lpbf-process-parameter-optimization-abstract.md#pre-submission-checklist-per-tms-abstract-guidelines)).

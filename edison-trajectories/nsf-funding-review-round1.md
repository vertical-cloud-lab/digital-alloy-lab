## Issues Identified (What Prevents A+)

### MEDIUM Severity

**1. DMREF international collaboration countries vs. Adam Clare (UK)**
- The document lists DMREF's international collaboration tracks as Israel, India, Canada, and Germany (line 81), then suggests leveraging "Adam Clare at U of Nottingham for UK/European connections" (lines 81, 91).
- **Problem**: The UK is NOT listed among DMREF's partner countries. The current wording implies UK is a DMREF track, which would be inaccurate and could lead to misdirected collaboration planning.
- **Fix**: Rewrite to clarify that Clare's value is as a bridge to German collaborators or European AM networks, not as a direct UK–DMREF connection. Or verify whether UK has been added to DMREF's international tracks under NSF 25-508.

**2. Non-NSF programs in Priority Matrix lack context**
- DARPA DSO BAA (#2), AFOSR YIP (#9), DARPA YFA (#10), ARO YIP (#14), and DoD DURIP (#15) appear in the priority matrix but have zero description in this document — that's 5 of 20 ranked programs (25%).
- **Fix**: Add a brief "Previously Documented Non-NSF Programs" summary table with one-line descriptions, or at minimum a prominent cross-reference note at the top of the priority matrix.

### LOW Severity

**3. Missing overlap/conflict analysis**
- No discussion of how simultaneous submissions to DOE Genesis Mission, NSF CAREER, DMREF, FM, CDS&E, and DMR TMRP will be differentiated. NSF prohibits substantially similar proposals to different programs, and Current & Pending/Other Support disclosure requirements apply.
- **Fix**: Add a brief section on scope differentiation strategy.

**4. CDS&E missing star emoji (⭐) in section header**
- CDS&E is one of 5 "key discoveries" in the Summary of Findings but lacks the ⭐ in its section header, unlike DMREF, FM, GOALI, and IRES.
- **Fix**: Add ⭐ to Section 3 header.

**5. October 2026 submission bottleneck not flagged**
- Three overlapping submission windows (CDS&E Oct 15, IRES Oct 26, MRI Oct 15–Nov 16) plus DARPA YFA in ~6 weeks. The timeline lists them but doesn't flag the resource constraint or suggest prioritization.
- **Fix**: Add a triage note (e.g., CDS&E and IRES highest priority; MRI could be deferred if equipment is funded through Genesis Mission).

**6. No mention of EPSCoR eligibility**
- Utah is an EPSCoR-eligible state. Several NSF programs have EPSCoR set-asides or preferences. Not mentioned anywhere.
- **Fix**: Add a note about Utah's EPSCoR status and any relevant set-asides.

### MINOR

**7. GOALI URL format** differs from all other entries (nsf.gov/eng/goali vs. nsf.gov/funding/opportunities/...). Should be verified given NSF's ongoing website reorganization.

**8. Future Manufacturing timeline note**: By March–April 2026, more should be known about whether a FY2026 FM solicitation exists. The "not yet confirmed" language is appropriately cautious but could be updated.

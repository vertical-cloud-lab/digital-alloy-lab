Question: How far apart must neighbouring single laser powder bed fusion tracks on the same powder layer be, and in what order and direction should they be exposed, so that each track behaves as if it were alone on an undisturbed powder layer? Context: we are planning single-track laser powder bed fusion (PBF-LB/M, L-PBF) experiments in 316L stainless steel on a Concept Laser M2 cusing (continuous-wave Yb fibre laser at about 1070 nm, 200 W or 400 W depending on configuration, focused spot about 50-90 um 1/e^2 diameter, galvanometer scanner, nitrogen or argon shielding gas flowing across the plate). Commercial gas-atomized 316L powder (about 15-45 um) is spread as ONE powder layer (nominally 30-50 um) on 316L substrate panels (roughly 80 mm square, bolted flush into a 250 mm build plate), and each track is a single scan vector with its own laser power P and scan speed v (P about 50-400 W, v about 0.1-3 m/s). Each build holds tens to about a hundred tracks. After the build we cut the panels with a bandsaw and a precision abrasive saw, grind to a section plane perpendicular to the scan direction (a transverse cross-section through all tracks in a row at once), mount, polish, etch and image in an SEM, and measure melt-pool width, depth below the substrate surface, height above it and area, and classify the regime (lack of fusion/discontinuous, balling, conduction, transition, keyhole). The measured cross-sections will be compared with STEADY-STATE single-track predictions of the Thermo-Calc Additive Manufacturing (AM) Module (CALPHAD-derived properties, Gaussian or keyhole heat source with absorptivity to calibrate), and with the analytical Eagar-Tsai model (travelling Gaussian surface source, constant properties). We want to calibrate the model's heat-source parameters on part of the tracks and validate on the rest.

**Summary of core recommendations for 316L, 50–400 W, 0.1–3 m/s, 30–50 µm layer, 1 atm:**
1. **Minimum spacing:** 3 mm default (use s ≥ W + 2d where d is denudation per side ~0.5–2 mm); increase to 4–5 mm for P ≥ 300 W at v ≤ 0.3 m/s
2. **Track length:** 15 mm; section near mid-length
3. **Scan direction:** Against gas flow (outlet → inlet)
4. **Exposure order:** Downstream (outlet) side first, progressing upstream toward inlet; interlace so consecutive tracks are ≥10 mm apart
5. **Randomization:** Randomize (P,v) assignment to positions using a blocked design; do not randomize scan sequence arbitrarily
6. **Edge margin:** ≥3 mm from panel edges
7. **Tracks per row:** ~24 on an 80 mm panel at 3 mm spacing
8. **Thermal interaction:** Negligible at ≥2–3 mm spacing (temperature rise ~1–20 K, ≪ melting point)
9. **The binding constraint is denudation, not thermal interaction:** waiting time alone cannot restore depleted powder

| Lack of fusion—vertical penetration | Flagged when melt-pool depth \(D \le t\), equivalently acceptable penetration requires \(D/t>1\) under the stated convention. | A pool that does not penetrate through the powder layer cannot reliably remelt and bond to the preceding solid layer, leaving unmolten material or interlayer voids. | Geometric overlap/penetration criteria are associated with Tang, Pistorius, and Beuth (2017) and related Beuth-group work. | Equality handling and whether Thermo-Calc uses any safety factor beyond unity should be checked against the applicable software-version documentation. |

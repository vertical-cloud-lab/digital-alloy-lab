## artifact-01

| Criterion name | Definition | Threshold used by the AM Module | Physical basis | Documented or proposed origin |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Keyhole porosity | Aspect ratio of the **vapour cavity**, where `Wk` is the calculated keyhole-cavity width and `Dk` its depth. It is **not** the melt-pool ratio `W/D` or `D/W`; a broad melt pool can contain a deep, narrow cavity. | `Wk/Dk ≤ 0.7` | A sufficiently deep, narrow vapour cavity is prone to wall/rear-front instability and collapse, which can trap gas as keyhole pores. | Thermo-Calc AM Module documentation and examples specify the `0.7` criterion. Its precise derivation is not disclosed in the accessible material; attribution to a particular Fabbro-type stability equation would therefore be inference rather than a documented fact. |
| Lack of fusion—layer penetration | `D` is the maximum liquidus-isotherm melt-pool depth below the current surface, and `t` is the deposited layer thickness. | Defect when `D/t ≤ 1` (equivalently `D ≤ t`) | The pool does not penetrate through the powder layer far enough to remelt and metallurgically bond to the preceding layer. | Thermo-Calc geometric printability criterion; this is a necessary penetration rule rather than a universal experimentally fitted porosity threshold. |
| Lack of fusion—inter-track overlap | `Dh` is the melt depth at a transverse offset equal to half the hatch spacing, `y = h/2`; it tests the location midway between neighboring scan tracks. | Defect when `Dh/t ≤ 1` (equivalently `Dh ≤ t`). A simpler width-only surrogate is inadequate overlap when `W < h`. | Even if the track center penetrates the layer, insufficient depth at the midpoint between tracks leaves an unmolten or incompletely remelted region. | Thermo-Calc geometric hatch-overlap criterion. It is more informative than `W ≥ h` because it evaluates penetration at the overlap location, but it remains an idealized geometric rule. |
| Balling | Slenderness of the calculated molten track, usually expressed using melt-pool length `L` and width `W`; this concerns the **liquid track**, not the keyhole cavity. | Thermo-Calc examples commonly use the default `L/W ≥ 2.3`; the installed-version documentation should be checked because criterion definitions or defaults can be version-dependent. | A long, narrow liquid track is susceptible to capillary breakup associated with Plateau–Rayleigh instability; wetting, contact angle, solidification time, and Marangoni flow can shift the actual boundary. | Thermo-Calc documentation presents `L/W` as an engineering screening criterion. The numerical threshold is empirical/reduced-order and should not be interpreted as the universal inviscid-cylinder Plateau–Rayleigh limit. |


*Table: The table distinguishes vapour-cavity keyholing from melt-pool geometry and summarizes the depth, hatch-overlap, and balling tests used for AM Module printability maps. It also separates documented Thermo-Calc rules from physical interpretation or inference.*

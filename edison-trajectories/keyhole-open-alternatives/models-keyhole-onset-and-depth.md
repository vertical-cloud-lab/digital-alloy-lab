# Open-Source Models for Keyhole-Mode Prediction in LPBF Printability Maps

## 1. Dimensionless Scaling Laws and Onset/Porosity Criteria

**Hann–King Normalized Enthalpy** (Hann et al. 2011, doi:10.1088/0022-3727/44/44/445401; King et al. 2014, doi:10.1063/1.4885144): ΔH/h_s = ηP/[ρCₚ(T_m−T₀)√(παvσ³)]. Keyhole onset at ΔH/h_s ≈ πT_b/T_m (~6 for 316L, ~9–10 for Al). Onset only, not depth. Strongly supported for steel/Ti; weakly for Al.

**Gan Ke Number** (Gan et al. 2021, doi:10.1038/s41467-021-22704-0): Best available universal onset + porosity classifier. Thresholds ~6–20 (convention-dependent). Strongly supported by multi-alloy synchrotron X-ray. Does not predict dimensions.

**Cunningham Power-Density** (2019, doi:10.1126/science.aav4687): ~10⁶ W/cm² onset for Ti-6Al-4V. Not transferable to Al without recalibration.

**Rubenchik–Wu–Mitchell / Ye** (2018, doi:10.1016/j.addma.2018.04.009; 2019, doi:10.1016/j.addma.2019.100933): Normalized depth d*=d/σ vs Pe. One of few fast models giving dimensional depth. Must recalibrate for Al-Si.

**Fabbro Depth** (2010, doi:10.1088/0022-3727/43/44/445501): d ≈ η_eff·P/[2πk(T_v−T₀)·√(1+vd/2α)]. Predicts depth analytically. Millisecond runtime.

**Printability Criteria** (Johnson 2019, doi:10.1016/j.addma.2019.100910; Seede 2020, doi:10.1016/j.actamat.2020.03.023): W/D≤2.5 or T_max≥T_b from conduction models. Structurally limited — retain only as heuristic.

## 2. Semi-Analytical Keyhole Models

**Kaplan (1994)** (doi:10.1088/0022-3727/27/9/002): Point-by-point energy balance with Fresnel absorption. Full keyhole profile. Steel errors ~10–30%. No public code; re-implementable. Seconds per point.

**Lankalapalli (1996)** (doi:10.1007/BF02659483): Simplified line-source keyhole. No public code.

## 3. Open-Source CFD Codes

**laserbeamFoam** (https://github.com/laserbeamfoam/LaserbeamFoam): GPL, VOF+ray tracing+recoil+evaporation. Best open-source keyhole solver. ~10–20% Ti-6Al-4V depth accuracy. ~100–1000 core-hours/mm.

**MOOSE** (https://github.com/idaholab/moose): LGPL-2.1. Flexible multiphysics framework. No turnkey keyhole application.

**AdditiveFOAM** (https://github.com/ORNL/AdditiveFOAM): Primarily thermal. Not a keyhole solver.

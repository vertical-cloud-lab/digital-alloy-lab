- **ARMD → AATT (NASA LaRC-centric)**: Multiple NASA Technical Publications on high-strength Al alloy AM (Al 7075 and Al-Cu-Zn-Mg) cite the **Advanced Air Transport Technology (AATT)** project as the core programmatic sponsor, consistent with aeronautics lightweighting and manufacturability thrusts.

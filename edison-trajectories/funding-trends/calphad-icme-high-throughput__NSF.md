### 3) Newer programs (launched 2022+) visibly funding the topic
- A clear “newer-award-number” signal is **DMREF-2323611**, acknowledged in 2025 work combining visualization + high-throughput CALPHAD in MPEA/HEA design spaces. (vela2025visualizinghighentropy pages 20-22)
- A workshop/roadmap award **DMR 2238231** (associated with a 2022 NSF-sponsored workshop leading to a 2025 MRS Bulletin article) suggests NSF investments in community planning for “materials laboratories of the future” aligned with MGI/autonomy themes. (banerjee2025materialslaboratoriesof pages 16-16)

### 4) Visible gaps and proposal opportunities (NSF → DOE Genesis/FLAIME relevance)
- The sampled acknowledgements show strong NSF support for **components** of accelerated discovery—thermodynamic/CALPHAD-informed modeling (DMR/CMMI), design-space exploration (DMREF), and compute (ACI/OAC)—but fewer explicit examples of **end-to-end autonomous experimental closed loops** (robotics + decision-making + synthesis/characterization + CALPHAD/ICME model updating) in structural alloys. (priyadarshini2025machinelearningdrivenclosedloop pages 20-23, banerjee2025materialslaboratoriesof pages 16-16)
- This gap suggests an opportunity for proposals emphasizing **tight integration** of CALPHAD/ICME digital twins with high-throughput experimentation and autonomous planning, potentially positioned at the intersection of DMREF-style design, CMMI manufacturing relevance, and cyberinfrastructure-enabled workflows. (kalidindi2022digitaltwinsfor pages 12-13, attari2022machinelearningassistedhighthroughput pages 17-19, wang2022designmetastabilityin pages 11-12)

## 4. BUDGET ARITHMETIC

**Does BYU $520K + partners = $750K? NO.**

Template illustrative budget (L724-741):
- BYU column: $330K + $55K + $30K + $0 + $8K + $0 + $7K + $90K = **$520,000** ✅ (matches stated)
- Total column: categories (partner subaward amount removed) = **$650,000** ❌ (stated as $750,000)
- Shortfall: **$100,000**

The gap is caused by the Indirect Costs (F&A) line being set at $100K in the Total column when the detailed budget document shows ~$200,610 in F&A to reach $750K. Additional missing line items (Equipment $12K, Software $18K, Publication $8K) account for $38K but are partially subsumed in the existing categories.

**Recommended fix:** Update the Indirect Costs row from BYU $90K / Total $100K to BYU $170K / Total $200K. This makes the BYU column sum to $600K and Total to $750K, consistent with the BYU total (from title page) = BYU direct + F&A.

# Qualification of additively manufactured copper alloys for ships

**Bottom line.** There is no verified, universal acceptance value for density, CT-detected pore size, corrosion current, or seawater corrosion rate that automatically qualifies a blown-powder laser-DED copper-alloy part or repair. Approval depends on the component, purchaser, alloy grade, process and essential variables, substrate, and applicable Navy or classification-society requirements. Published WAAM propellers and laser-clad propeller specimens provide useful *comparators*, not approval of a new single-stream powder alloy. The original ABS, Lloyd’s Register, NAVSEA propeller-repair, and most proprietary standards were not available in the retrieved literature; their clause-level requirements should not be represented as verified below. (govindaraj2025characterisationofmarine pages 21-26, harwig2024techpubqualification pages 3-5, ziabari20262026ndefor pages 53-54)

**Scope of the numbers below.** Compositions are wt%; Cu is the balance where stated. “Not reported” means that the cited work cannot establish the requested measurement, not that a qualifying authority waives it. None of the literature values below is a verified acceptance limit for the proposed laser-DED process.

## 1. Governing frameworks and acceptance tests

1. **DNV.** DNV, *DNV-ST-B203, Additive Manufacturing of Metallic Parts*, October 2022; and *DNV-CG-0197, Additive Manufacturing—Qualification and Certification Process for Materials and Components*, October 2021. Related propeller documents are *DNV-RU-SHIP*, Part 2, Chapter 2, Section 11, copper-alloy propeller castings, and *DNV-CG-0039*, calculation of marine propellers. **DOI:** none verified for these standards. **Process:** metal AM, with cast NAB as an application-specific comparator. The retrieved literature describes design, feedstock, equipment, process, testing, inspection, and audit controls, with risk-dependent certification; it does **not** reproduce B203’s copper-alloy chemistry, CT, density, tensile, Charpy, or corrosion acceptance clauses. A published CU3 cast-propeller comparison lists yield ≥250 MPa, UTS ≥600 MPa, elongation ≥13%, and hardness ≥140 HBW; these are identified as **cast-CU3 comparators**, not transferable B203 limits for a powder-DED repair. **Tags:** [CITE] [NUMBERS] [GAP]. 2. **ABS and Lloyd’s Register.** ABS guidance on additive manufacturing and Lloyd’s Register’s marine AM approval framework are relevant to manufacturer/process approval and component-specific certification, but their controlling editions, exact titles, DOI status, and copper-alloy acceptance tables could **not** be authenticated in the retrieved source texts. Do not assign either society a purported “≥99% density,” fixed CT pore limit, mandatory Charpy energy, or seawater-current threshold. **Process:** qualification must distinguish powder-laser DED from WAAM, LPBF, casting, and repair welding; a society’s approval of one does not qualify another. **Tags:** [CITE—primary documents needed] [GAP]. The documented DNV-oriented marine qualification account supports the distinction between manufacturer approval, production destructive/NDT acceptance, and a mere successful material experiment. ## 3. Defensible acceptance-test package and university contribution

**Specify the acceptance authority first.** A ship repair needs an agreed component drawing and loading/service envelope; designated base-metal and deposit specifications; an approved powder lot and AM procedure; representative free-standing and *both* substrate-repair coupons; an inspectable production geometry; and purchaser-authorized property and flaw limits. Test orientation, thickness, surface condition, and process start/stops matter. A successful small coupon alone is not a valve, deck drain, or propeller repair qualification. The published Navy arc-DED study’s wall/block, multiple-orientation strategy is directly useful as a *plan*, although its stainless-steel results cannot qualify copper. (harwig2024techpubqualification pages 3-5, harwig2024techpubqualification pages 5-7, ziabari20262026ndefor pages 53-54, govindaraj2025characterisationofmarine pages 21-26)

The following matrix separates measurements a university can reasonably supply from approvals that require a Navy, shipyard, class society, or accredited laboratory. Its numeric examples are **explicitly comparators, not proposed mandatory limits**. (ziabari20262026ndefor pages 53-54, govindaraj2025characterisationofmarine pages 26-31, fayanas2025nickelaluminiumbronze pages 1-2)

In particular, measure **both powder and deposited chemistry**, including Ni/Fe/Mn/Cr/Al and oxygen where relevant; report powder particle-size distribution, Hall flow and apparent density **per lot**, rather than adopting the published 45–150 µm size as a requirement. Use Archimedes, sectioning, and resolution-qualified CT to quantify distinct gas pores, lack-of-fusion defects, and cracks; obtain written application-specific acceptance thresholds rather than labeling “99% dense” qualified. Measure deposit and transverse *repair-interface* tensile response, guided bends, and—in a propeller or other impact-relevant thick component—Charpy with agreed notch and temperature; apply qualified surface and volumetric NDE to the representative geometry. For the stated seawater goal, test **as-deposited** free-standing material, NAB-on-cast-NAB repairs, Cu-Ni-on-wrought-Cu-Ni repairs, and a **same-run wrought C71500 control**, with the *same* finish, area, temperature, aeration, electrolyte, exposure and hydrodynamics. Distinguish inexpensive 3.5 wt% NaCl electrochemistry from **ASTM D1141 substitute seawater** and from **flowing natural seawater**. A 30-day immersion with mass change, repeated OCP/EIS, cross-sectional *maximum selective-attack depth*, and interface examination is a suitable project milestone—not a demonstrated DNV/ABS/NAVSEA mandatory test. Flowing seawater, pressure/leak testing for valves or drains, service-life substantiation, independent NDE and final ship installation authorization warrant a qualified partner. ### Top 5 things the PI should know

1. **Do not call a research coupon “ship-qualified.”** Establish the purchaser, applicable NAVSEA/class rules, alloy grade, repair geometry, and approved acceptance matrix before selecting targets; the verified AM-specific Navy publication is **wire-arc**, not the proposed powder-laser process. (harwig2024techpubqualification pages 1-3, ziabari20262026ndefor pages 53-54, govindaraj2025characterisationofmarine pages 21-26)
2. **The most threatening prior art is pre-alloyed powder laser cladding on a cast, low-Ni bronze propeller.** Its reported clad current is **18.7% higher than cast**; the credible distinction is improved *as-deposited*, quantitatively verified seawater response on **both repair substrates**, not merely printing a bronze alloy. (ziqiang2025lasercladdingremanufacturing pages 1-3, ziqiang2025lasercladdingremanufacturing pages 6-9, ziqiang2025lasercladdingremanufacturing pages 3-6)
3. **WAAM has already reported a mechanically strong full-size NAB propeller and superior as-built NAB chloride behavior.** Separate the new single-powder laser/feedstock-and-thermal-history contribution from those wire-arc achievements, and never substitute their short static NaCl results for 30-day flowing seawater. (shahriari2023corrosionperformanceof pages 14-14, shahriari2023corrosionperformanceof pages 2-2, govindaraj2025characterisationofmarine pages 26-31)
4. **Acceptance values are grade- and contract-specific.** The **250/600/13/140** cast-CU3 comparison and **280/650/13** EN 1982 comparison refer to different comparators; neither specifies a universal laser-DED yield, density, CT defect, Charpy, or corrosion limit. (govindaraj2025characterisationofmarine pages 26-31, govindaraj2025characterisationofmarine media 58b9b86a, fayanas2025nickelaluminiumbronze pages 2-5)
5. **A university can deliver a compelling *prequalification* package:** traceable atomization lots; measured chemistry/oxygen/size/flow; self-build and both substrate-interface soundness; directional mechanical/NDE data; and matched as-deposited C71500 seawater comparisons. Accredited acceptance, production representativeness, pressure or propeller fitness-for-service, and authorization to install remain partner responsibilities. (harwig2024techpubqualification pages 3-5, ziabari20262026ndefor pages 53-54, govindaraj2025characterisationofmarine pages 21-26, fayanas2025nickelaluminiumbronze pages 1-2)

## Discretionary Analytical Decisions

- **Graded the plan as a proposal population guide**, not as the final proposal narrative. Issues were evaluated based on their potential impact on the submitted proposal if propagated during execution.
- **Assessed figure space at ~1.6-2.0 pages** using the plan's own estimates as lower bound and adding ~0.2-0.4 pages for realistic figure rendering with captions, panel labels, and LaTeX float spacing. Different LaTeX compilers and figure aspect ratios could shift this by ±0.1 pages.
- **Weighted the backronym inconsistency as CRITICAL** rather than MEDIUM because the project title appears on every page (header), the title page, the abstract, and is the first element reviewers encounter. A title misaligned with the topic ("Materials Engineering" vs. "Mineral Efficiency") signals lack of attention to the specific FOA topic.
- **Assessed the discovery yield discrepancy as CRITICAL** because having two different targets (3% vs 20%) in different authoritative documents risks the writing team using the wrong number, which would either undercommit (3%) or overcommit (20%) in front of reviewers.
- **Used ~500 words/page** as the standard conversion factor for 11pt Times Roman with 1-inch margins, consistent with the plan's own estimate and standard DOE proposal formatting.
- **Rated competitive positioning at B+ rather than A-** because the UG-heavy team structure and early-career PI profile represent significant reviewer concerns that the plan addresses only defensively, not offensively.
- **Treated AMAZEMET/Thermo-Calc budget categorization as HIGH priority** because the distinction between subaward and direct cost affects F&A calculations, cost share requirements, and the 2-of-3 team composition argument — all of which have cascading effects on multiple proposal sections.
- **Evaluated against the 2022 USGS Critical Minerals List** (50 minerals) as the reference for Mn/Cr/Zr criticality claims, consistent with the plan's own citations and the most recent finalized list at time of analysis.
- **Assessed the project overview paragraph as MEDIUM priority** despite its strategic importance, because the plan provides adequate content bullets — the gap is in polished draft text rather than missing information.
- **Estimated fix times based on single-person editing** assuming familiarity with all source documents. Collaborative editing or unfamiliarity could increase times by 2-3×.

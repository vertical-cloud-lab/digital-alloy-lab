# External Evaluator Slate for an Early-Career Autonomous Alloy Discovery Program

## Overview

This document identifies the strongest external evaluators—letter writers, advisory-board candidates, panel reviewers, program-officer contacts, and anti-recommendations—for a single-PI early-career research program centered on Bayesian Optimization of Function Networks (BOFN/pKGFN) with integrated CALPHAD simulation, Scheil-Gulliver solidification modeling, and LPBF additive manufacturing for closed-loop alloy discovery. The evaluators are organized across five sections, with coverage spanning all three target funding programs (AFOSR YIP, DOE ECRP, NSF CAREER) and balancing geographic, institutional, and demographic diversity.

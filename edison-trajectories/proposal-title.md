## 1. DOE Proposal Title Conventions

> *Sections 1–6 document the original Topic 12F title research. They remain valuable as DOE convention reference. For the active Topic 3B title, see Section 9.*

Edison Q1 analysis of funded DOE projects across ARPA-E, AMMTO, BES, and national lab LDRD programs revealed dominant title structures:

| Program Type | Typical Structure | Word Count | Key Conventions |
|---|---|---:|---|
| **ARPA-E** | `Acronym: Descriptive Technical Subtitle` | 8–15 | Memorable ALL-CAPS acronyms (METALS, ULTIMATE); action-oriented, application-forward |
| **AMMTO / Genesis** | Descriptive title centered on materials/manufacturing objective | 10–18 | Signals P-S-P-P framework, rapid qualification, AI/ML, LPBF, manufacturing readiness |
| **BES** | Fully descriptive, science-first | 10–15 | Fundamental-science tone; mechanisms, synthesis, AI/ML-enabled discovery |
| **ORNL LDRD** | `Method/Capability for Target Application` | 8–16 | Novel capability, instrument, or workflow; concise and specific |
| **LLNL LDRD** | Descriptive methodology-focused | 10–20 | Strong use of "Self-Driving," "Autonomous," "AI-Assisted," "Rapid Design-to-Deployment" |

**Best practice:** The "ACRONYM: Descriptive Subtitle" format is the dominant convention when a memorable project name exists. It creates brand recognition, allows the subtitle to contain AMMTO keywords, and signals a cohesive named platform.

### High-Priority AMMTO Keywords

From the AMMT 2025 Roadmap's four pillars (Advanced Materials & Manufacturing, Rapid Qualification, Environmental Effects, Technology Maturation):

- *Accelerated materials development/discovery*
- *Predictable functionality* (directly from Genesis Mission Topic 12)
- *Processing-Structure-Property-Performance (P-S-P-P)*
- *Rapid qualification / accelerated qualification*
- *AI/ML-enabled* and *ICME*
- *High-throughput testing / accelerated testing*
- *Laser powder bed fusion (LPBF)*
- *Manufacturing readiness / technology maturation*
- *Closed-loop / autonomous*
- *Powder-to-part / design-to-deployment*

### Title Length

Recommended optimal: **11–15 words** in the subtitle (12–16 total including acronym). This balances specificity with readability across DOE programs.

### Qualification in Titles

- "Qualification" signals manufacturing readiness — a strong positive for AMMTO
- "Validation" pairs well with "predictable functionality" or "predictable performance"
- For early-TRL projects: "toward qualification" or "qualification-ready" are appropriate hedges
- "Predictable functionality" is the AMMTO-native equivalent that implicitly encompasses qualification intent

### Mechanism vs. BOFN Emphasis: Which Should Lead?

Edison recommends **hybrid** (10/10 RFA alignment) — BOFN as the engine that *enables* mechanism discovery, not an optimization tool:

| Strategy | RFA Score | Key Strength | Key Risk |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Mechanism-focused | 9/10 | Strongest direct 11C fit; centers PSP science | Reviewers may question AI novelty depth |
| BOFN/methodology-focused | 5/10 | Clear AI novelty story | Reads as optimization, not understanding; BES PM may redirect to AMMTO |
| **Hybrid** | **10/10** | **BOFN DAG mirrors causal chain; intermediate nodes expose model-discrepancy signals** | **Hardest to write well; requires crisp scientific questions** |

**Key insight from Edison:** BOFN's DAG structure is an isomorphism of the causal process chain (composition → powder → melt pool → solidification → microstructure → properties). Each intermediate node produces observables that can be compared against physics-based predictions. When GP posteriors diverge from physics models, that constitutes a model-discrepancy signal — exactly the "phenomena not consistent with current scientific models" that 11C calls for. BOFN is reframed as a *structured inference engine for autonomous hypothesis generation*, not a faster optimizer.

### Strategic Framing Guidance

Edison's single most important recommendation: **Invert the narrative hierarchy.** For 12F the alloy is the deliverable and AI is the tool. For 11C the scientific understanding is the deliverable and alloys are the demonstration vehicle.

The proposal must include explicit mechanisms for:
1. **Model-discrepancy detection** — comparing BOFN intermediate-node observations against physics-model predictions
2. **Hypothesis generation** — translating detected discrepancies into testable hypotheses
3. **Hypothesis testing** — directing acquisition functions to explore discrepancy regions
4. **Interpretable model extraction** — building human-readable, physics-linked explanations

**Misconception to avoid:** Do not frame the proposal as "we optimize alloys and learn things along the way." BES funds the *production* of scientific understanding, not optimization with serendipitous insights.

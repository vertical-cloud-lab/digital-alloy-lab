## 1. Superheat at the 1300 C setpoint

- Cu-30Ni (what #219 assumed): liquidus 1240 C, so **60 C** of superheat at 1300 C.

## 2. Melt composition in atom fractions

Nominal Cu-10Al-5Ni-3Fe (wt%): x_Cu = 0.717, x_Al = 0.206, x_Ni = 0.047, x_Fe = 0.030.

## 3. Tungsten solubility: C63000 against Cu-30Ni

- C63000: z = Ni/(Ni+Cu) = 0.062, x_W = 8.37e-06, about **0.0024 wt% W** (24 ppm by mass) at saturation.
- Cu-30Ni: z = Ni/(Ni+Cu) = 0.317, x_W = 2.31e-03, about **0.68 wt% W** (6813 ppm by mass) at saturation.

The fit was measured for z = 0.054-0.368, so C63000 (z = 0.062) is inside it. It covers Cu-Ni only: the 3% Fe (Fe dissolves W) and the 10% Al (Al-W intermetallics) would raise the C63000 value, but not plausibly by the factor of ~300 between the two alloys. The W-based coating in AMAZEMET's 2026 plate study is a Si-W infiltration layer of W5Si3 + WC rather than metallic W (doi:10.3390/ma19132803); if our plate is that kind, this bounds dissolution of metallic W only.

## 4. Does the Al in the melt attack graphite, BN or silica at 1300 C?

Threshold Al activity for each reaction, from Delta G at 1573 K (liquid Al reference):

- 4 Al + 3 C -> Al4C3: Delta G = -114 kJ/mol, so it needs **a_Al > 0.11**.
- Al + BN -> AlN + B (pure B): Delta G = -32 kJ/mol, so it needs **a_Al > 0.09**. If the boron dissolves at a_B ~ 0.01, the threshold drops to ~0.0009, so a thin AlN/boride reaction film and a little B pickup are possible.
- 4/3 Al + SiO2 -> 2/3 Al2O3 + Si: Delta G = -152 kJ per mol SiO2, so it runs at any plausible a_Al: silica in a binder or on the plate coating ends up as dissolved Si.

Estimated a_Al in the melt: liquid Cu-Al mixes very exothermically (integral enthalpy minimum about -18 to -20 kJ/mol near x_Al = 0.35-0.4). As a regular solution, Omega = -75 kJ/mol; with G_excess between 0.6 and 1.0 of H_excess,
- G_ex/H_ex = 1.0: gamma_Al = 0.027, **a_Al = 0.006**
- G_ex/H_ex = 0.6: gamma_Al = 0.114, **a_Al = 0.024**

Ni and Fe bind Al further (NiAl, FeAl), so a_Al ~ 0.005-0.02 is 4-20x below the Al4C3 and AlN thresholds: the graphite crucible, the BN coat and rod, and the carbon-fiber plate should not form Al4C3 or bulk AlN, whereas silica and B2O3 binders will be reduced.

## 2. INCORRECT ITEMS

**Verdict: No major factual errors. Two items need clarification:**

1. **Letter confusion in B.3 (Lines 211–212):** Valimet appears in both the "Letters of Commitment" list AND the "Letters of Collaboration" list. While a partner can theoretically need both (commitment = institutional binding agreement signed by authorized official; collaboration = 2-sentence format per FOA p.64-65), this dual listing is confusing. Recommendation: clarify that Valimet needs a Letter of **Commitment** (as funded subaward partner) and that the collaboration letter is the separate 2-sentence format document. For AMAZEMET and Thermo-Calc, since they are BYU direct costs (not subaward partners), verify whether the strict 2-sentence collaboration format is required or if Letters of Support suffice.

2. **AMAZEMET location inconsistency:** The checklist says "Warsaw, Poland" (line 214), which matches genesis-mission-gotchas.md and budget-assessment-official-requirements.md. However, proposal-population-plan.md says "Białystok, Poland" (Appendix 6 section). One of these is wrong — verify AMAZEMET's actual registered address for the foreign connections disclosure.

3. **Minor: Appendix 4 checkbox (line 125)** could be misread as a submission requirement. While the "NOT required at Phase I submission" bold note is clear, having a checkbox for a non-required item may cause confusion during rapid reference. Consider using a different symbol (e.g., [ ] → [N/A]) or moving to a "post-award planning" sub-section.

## Discretionary Analytical Decisions

- **Graded the checklist on a holistic A-F scale rather than a numerical rubric** — multiple grading systems are valid; the A-F scale was chosen to match the Edison review trajectory already established across rounds
- **Weighted "usability during active drafting" heavily** in the overall grade — some reviewers might weight completeness more; the A grade for usability reflects the practical value of the Quick-Reference table and source citations for a time-pressured proposal writer
- **Classified the Valimet letter dual-listing as "confusing but not necessarily incorrect"** rather than a clear error — the FOA may indeed require both types from the same partner, but a more conservative reviewer might flag this as an error
- **Classified NEPA, export control, IP provisions, and lobbying certification as LOW/LOW-MEDIUM missing items** — a compliance-focused reviewer might rate these higher, particularly export control given the foreign partners; I weighted them lower because they are post-award or form-embedded items unlikely to cause a desk rejection
- **Treated the AMAZEMET Warsaw vs. Białystok discrepancy as a "verify" item rather than a clear error** — without authoritative verification, I flagged the inconsistency across documents but did not declare either location correct
- **Set the Topic 3-B specificity grade at B+ rather than B** — the scope, budget, and team compliance items are all accurate; the missing items (CMS pillars, EO 14017, alloy families) are strategic enhancements rather than compliance gaps
- **Considered but did not separately grade the FAQ section (E) and Award Mechanism section (F)** — these are reference material rather than compliance checklists, so they were evaluated as supporting the overall completeness and accuracy grades

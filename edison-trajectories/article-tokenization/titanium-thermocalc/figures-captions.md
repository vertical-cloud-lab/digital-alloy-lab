## FIGURES
Figure 1: Vertical section of Al-2.25Cu-1.8Mg-xTi alloys.
Figure 2: (a) T vs. (fs)1/2 curves of Al-Cu-Mg-xTi alloys with Ti contents ranging from 0 to 2 wt%; (b) calculated Qtrue values in Ti/Al-Cu-Mg alloys for constrained L→α-Al solidification with Ti contents ranging from 0 to 2 wt%. (Metastable solidification conditions are used at higher values of wt% Ti to suppress the formation of intermetallic phases.).
Figure 3: Solidification paths of (a) Al-Cu-Mg and (b) 1.5 wt% Ti/Al-Cu-Mg alloy with solid fraction using the Gulliver-Scheil model.
Figure 4: 3-D optical images of the etched structure of SLM-fabricated (a) Al-Cu-Mg alloy and (b) Ti/Al-Cu-Mg alloy samples. (Z represents the building direction.).
Figure 5: The microstructure of SLM-fabricated Al-Cu-Mg alloy. (a) cross-sectional SEM micrograph; (b) TEM bright-field image of network structure; (c) EDS element mapping of Al, Mg and Cu; (d) TEM micrograph of the Al2Cu compound; (e) HRTEM micrograph of the interface between Al2Cu and α-Al matrix with the corresponding FFT pattern. (For interpretation of the references to colour in this figure legend, the reader is referred to the web version of this article.)
Figure 6: The microstructure of SLM-fabricated Ti/Al-Cu-Mg alloy. (a) cross-sectional SEM micrograph; (b) TEM bright-field image showing an entire α-Al matrix cell; (c) EDS element mapping of Al, Mg, Cu and Ti corresponding to the yellow frame in b; (d) TEM micrograph of the Al3Ti phase with the corresponding SAED pattern; (e) HRTEM micrograph of the interface between Al3Ti phase and α-Al matrix. (For interpretation of the references to colour in this figure legend, the reader is referred to the web version of this article.)
Figure 7: EBSD orientation maps from side view of SLM-fabricated (a) Al-Cu-Mg and (b) Ti/Al-Cu-Mg samples; (c) grain size distributions; pole figures showing the orientations of the SLM-fabricated (d) Al-Cu-Mg and (e) Ti/Al-Cu-Mg alloy; and the misorientation angle distribution of (f) Al-Cu-Mg and (g) Ti/Al-Cu-Mg samples.
Figure 8: Mechanical properties of SLM-fabricated Al-Cu-Mg and Ti/Al-Cu-Mg alloys.
Figure 9: SEM micrographs of the fracture surfaces of the (a-b) Al-Cu-Mg alloy and (c-d) Ti/Al-Cu-Mg alloy after tensile tests.
Figure 10: Schematic diagram showing the formation process of the fine equiaxed grains of Ti/Al-Cu-Mg alloys.
Figure 11: (a) Schematic diagram of cracking mechanism in the Al-Cu-Mg alloy; (b) SEM image and (c) IPF map showing the microstructure in the cracked zone of Al-Cu-Mg alloy; (d) SEM image and (e) IPF map of Ti/Al-Cu-Mg alloy consisting of fine equiaxed grains without cracks.

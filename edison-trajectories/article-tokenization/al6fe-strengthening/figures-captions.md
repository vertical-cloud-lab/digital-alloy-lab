## FIGURES
Figure 1: (a) Alloy composition on a vertical section of Al–2.5Fe–xMn (wt%) obtained from the calculated Al–Fe–Mn ternary phase diagram and (b) changes in calculated phase fractions in equilibrium with temperature.
Figure 2: Optical micrographs showing melt pool structures of (a) laser powder bed fusion (L-PBF) fabricated Al–Fe–Mn alloy sample and (b-d) samples that were exposed to elevated temperatures for prolonged periods: (b) 200 ℃-1000 h, (c) 300 ℃-1000 h, and (d) 500 ℃-100 h.
Figure 3: Electron backscatter diffraction (EBSD) inverse pole figure orientation maps of the α-Al (face-centered cubic structure) matrix and measured equivalent grain size (deq) of (a) L-PBF-fabricated Al–Fe–Mn alloy sample and (b-d) samples that were thermally exposed to elevated temperatures for prolonged periods: (b) 200 ℃-1000 h, (c) 300 ℃-1000 h, and (d) 500 ℃-100 h. In these maps, the fine and bold lines represent misorientations (θ) of 2 < θ < 15º and θ > 15º, respectively.
Figure 4: Representative X-ray diffraction profiles of the L-PBF-fabricated Al–Fe–Mn alloy sample and those that were thermally exposed to elevated temperatures for prolonged periods.
Figure 5: Representative scanning electron microscopy (SEM) backscattered electron images (BEIs) showing microstructures around melt pool boundaries in (a) L-PBF-fabricated Al–Fe–Mn alloy sample and samples that were thermally exposed to elevated temperatures for prolonged periods: (b) 200 ℃-1000 h, (c) 300 ℃-1000 h, and (d) 500 ℃-100 h.
Figure 6: (a) Representative SEM BEIs showing microstructures of the L-PBF fabricated Al–Fe–Mn alloy sample after exposure to 500 ℃ for 100 h, (b) EBSD patterns obtained from the locations marked as 1, 2, and 3 in (a), and indexed EBSD patterns using a crystal structure of the Al6M (M = Fe or Mn) phase (oC28).
Figure 7: Representative bright-field transmission electron microscopy (TEM) images showing the morphologies of intermetallic phases in (a) L-PBF-fabricated Al–Fe–Mn alloy sample and those that were thermally exposed to elevated temperatures for prolonged periods: (b) 200 ℃-1000 h, (c) 300 ℃-1000 h, and (d) 500 ℃-100 h. These images were captured inside the melt pool.
Figure 8: (a, c) Representative bright-field TEM images of coarsened intermetallic phases and (b, d) corresponding selected-area electron diffraction patterns of the sample that was thermally exposed to 500 ℃ for 100 h.
Figure 9: Representative high-angle annular dark-field scanning TEM (HAADF-STEM) images and corresponding energy-dispersive X-ray spectroscopy maps of Fe, Mn, O, and Si elements in (a) L-PBF-fabricated Al–Fe–Mn alloy sample and (b, c) samples that were thermally exposed to elevated temperatures of 300 and 500 ℃ for prolonged periods. These images were captured inside the melt pool.
Figure 10: (a, c) Measured solute Fe and Mn contents in the α-Al matrix at different locations ((a, b) relatively coarsened microstructure regions localized at melt pool boundaries and (c, d) refined microstructure regions inside the melt pools) of the L-PBF-fabricated Al–Fe–Mn alloy sample and samples that were thermally exposed to various temperatures for prolonged periods and (b, d) corresponding HAADF-STEM images.
Figure 11: (a-f) Representative high-resolution SEM images showing the morphologies of intermetallic phases observed at different locations ((a-c) relatively coarsened microstructure regions localized at melt pool boundaries and (d-f) refined microstructure regions inside the melt pools) of the samples exposed to 300 ℃ for different periods. (g-i) Variations in morphological parameters as a function of exposure time: (g) area fraction, (h) average size, and (i) number density of the intermetallic phases.
Figure 12: (a-c) Representative high-resolution SEM images showing the morphologies of intermetallic phases in the samples exposed to 500 ℃ for different periods. (d-f) Variations in morphological parameters as a function of exposure time: (d) area fraction, (e) average size, and (f) number density of the intermetallic phases.
Figure 13: Variation in Vickers hardness of the L-PBF-fabricated Al–Fe–Mn ternary alloy sample as a function of exposure time at different temperatures.
Figure 14: Measured composition of the α-Al phase (in the sample exposed at 500 ℃ for 100 h) plotted on a calculated isothermal section at 500 ℃ of the Al–Fe–Mn ternary system. Solubility limits in each binary system [49,53] are plotted on the isothermal section.

Figure 1: Microstructures of the Al–Zn–Mg–Cu–Si alloy after step I aging (80 °C/36 h). (a) HAADF-STEM micrograph viewed along [001]Al zone axis. (b) A sub-volume (25 nm × 25 nm × 80 nm) showing the distribution of Mg (green), Zn (orange), Cu (dark blue), and Si (light blue) atoms in clusters. (c) Magnified inset of the box in (b), providing a clear view of the cluster distribution. (d) Number density of various solute clusters. (For interpretation of the references to colour in this figure legend, the reader is referred to the web version of this article.)
Figure 2: Microstructures of the Al–Zn–Mg–Cu–Si alloy after step II aging (150 °C/10 h). Bright field TEM (BF-TEM) micrographs viewed along (a) [110]Al and (b) [001]Al zone axes. High-resolution TEM (HR-TEM) micrographs showing (c) the η′ phase, (d) the T′ phase, and (e) the Si-rich phase precursors, with their respective FFT patterns displayed below. (f) 3D atom distributions of Mg (green), Zn (orange), Cu (dark blue), and Si (light blue) atoms within the selected volume (40 nm × 40 nm × 150 nm). (g) Iso-concentration surfaces of (f) with 9 at.% Zn (orange) and 1.0 at.% Si (light blue). (h) Atom distributions of the Si-rich clusters in the matrix corresponding to the blue box in (f). (i) Number density of various solute clusters in step II samples. (For interpretation of the references to colour in this figure legend, the reader is referred to the web version of this article.)
Figure 3: BF-TEM micrographs of (a–e) the Al–Zn–Mg–Cu–Si alloy after the multi-step aging, with (f–j) the single-step aged sample shown for comparison. (a,b) and (f,g) Low-magnification BF-TEM micrographs of the two samples viewed along [110]Al and [001]Al zone axes. η-type phases are indicated by blue arrows, T-type phases are circled with yellow dotted lines, and QP2 phases are marked by dark red arrows. (c–e) and (h–j) Magnified HR-TEM micrographs of the η′, T′, and QP2 phases, respectively, accompanied by their corresponding FFT patterns displayed below.
Figure 4: BF-TEM micrograph of the Al–Zn–Mg–Cu–Si alloy after thermal exposure at 150 °C for 500 h viewed along (a–c) [110]Al and (b–d) [001]Al zone axes, respectively. η-type phases are indicated by blue arrows, T-type phases are circled with yellow dotted lines, and QP2 phases are marked by dark red dotted lines. Magnified HR-TEM micrographs showing (b) the η′ phase, (c) the η phase, (e) the T′ phase, and (f) the QP2 phase, accompanied by their corresponding FFT patterns. Diameter distributions of (g) the η-type phase, (h) the T-type phase, and (i) the QP2 phase, as well as the length distribution for (j) the QP2 phase in the Al alloy before and after thermal exposure.
Figure 5: (a) Schematic diagram illustrating the microstructural features of the Al–Zn–Mg–Cu–Si alloy after each aging step and subsequent thermal exposure. (b) Engineering stress and strain curves of the Al–Zn–Mg–Cu–Si alloy in various conditions. (c) Yield strength and corresponding strength retention rates of Al–Zn–Mg(–Cu) and Al–Mg–Si(–Cu) series Al-based alloys after thermal exposure at 100 °C or 150 °C for 100 or 500 h [8,42,51–61].

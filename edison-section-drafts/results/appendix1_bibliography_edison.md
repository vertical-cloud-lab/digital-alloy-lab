## Important Notes for the PI

1. **AMMT Roadmap [16]**: The OSTI-confirmed document (ANL-24-70) is from DOE's Office of Nuclear Energy AMMT program, not EERE's AMMTO. If the intended reference is an EERE/AMMTO-specific additive manufacturing strategy document, the PI should verify the exact document and substitute accordingly. No standalone EERE/AMMTO AM roadmap was found in public databases.

2. **Khatamsaz et al. [7]**: The literature query confirmed the DOI as `10.1038/s41524-023-01006-7` (constrained MOBO for Mo-Nb-Ti-V-W MPEAs). A second Khatamsaz 2023 paper also exists in npj Comput. Mater. on NiTi shape memory alloys (`10.1038/s41524-023-01173-7`). The MPEA paper is the correct one for the cost-aware BO framing.

3. **Chawla et al. [10]**: This is an arXiv preprint (not yet peer-reviewed journal publication). If journal publication status changes before submission, update accordingly.

4. **Astudillo & Frazier**: Split into two entries [4] and [5] because the 2019 ICML paper (composite functions) and the 2021 NeurIPS paper (function networks/BOFN) are distinct contributions both central to the proposal. The pKGFN extension [6] is included as a third entry since it is the specific acquisition function used in FLAIME.

5. **Tom et al. [14]**: This is the 100-page Chemical Reviews SDL review where PI Baird is 3rd author and lead on the structural materials section — a key credibility reference.

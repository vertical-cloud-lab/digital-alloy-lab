## June 2026 Refresh

A fresh pass over the NSF opportunities catalog (and adjacent DOE/DoD vehicles) on **2026-06-20** updated the following and surfaced four newly relevant programs/watch items:

- **NSF Future Manufacturing (NSF 24-525)** — **no longer accepting proposals.** NSF closed the FY2025 competition (last extended deadline Jun 18, 2025) and, as of the May 2025 update, will **not** accept further proposals to NSF 24-525. NSF intends to issue a new manufacturing research/education opportunity in FY2026 "subject to availability of funds," but **no successor solicitation or deadline has been released as of Jun 2026.** Treat FM as "watch for successor," not a Jun 2026 target. ([FM updates](https://www.nsf.gov/funding/opportunities/fm-future-manufacturing/updates))
- **NSF DMREF (NSF 25-508)** — confirmed **biennial, odd-numbered years**; NSF 25-508's full-proposal date (Feb 4, 2025) has passed, so the **next competition is anticipated in early 2027**. The current solicitation now lists **DOE EERE, ONR, AFRL, NIST, and DEVCOM (GVSC/ARL)** as federal partners and **NSERC (Canada) and DFG (Germany)** as international partners — directly enabling the Canadian/German tracks Dr. Schlueter flagged.
- **NSF MRI (NSF 23-519)** — confirmed: **no FY2026 window**; next submission window is **Oct 15 – Nov 16, 2026** (FY2027). (Already reflected below.)
- **DARPA DSO Office-wide BAA** — the DSO office-wide BAA cycle referenced in the priority matrix **closed Jun 2, 2026**; the front door is currently **between cycles** (successor FY-cycle BAA not yet posted as of Jun 2026). The 1-page exec-summary route is temporarily unavailable — monitor [DARPA DSO opportunities](https://www.darpa.mil/research/offices/defense-sciences) / Grants.gov for the successor BAA before drafting. This shifts the recommended near-term action toward **NSF CAREER (Jul 22, 2026)** — see [next action](#highest-leverage-next-action-summer-2026).
- **NEW — NSF Materials Innovation Platforms (MIP, NSF 25-521).** Mid-scale materials infrastructure (~$16M FY26 for 1–3 awards); the 2025 cycle **explicitly targeted alloys, amorphous, and composite materials** (deadline May 15, 2025, passed). A near-perfect infrastructure vehicle for a closed-loop alloy-discovery hub — a **Priority-1 target if a future cycle keeps the alloy focus.**
- **NEW — DARPA SMART (Strategic Materials Accelerator & Research Testbed), U of Utah.** Announced May 2026; a DARPA-backed critical-minerals/REE testbed at the University of Utah (first program EMBER — biotech REE recovery from mining waste). Adjacent rather than a direct alloy-design call, but FLAIME's existing **U of Utah collaboration (Taylor Sparks)** is a potential entry point to this critical-minerals ecosystem and a leverage point for DARPA SURGE / AMMTO framing.
- **NEW — NSF Regional Innovation Engines (NSF Engines, NSF 24-565; TIP Directorate).** Place-based awards of **$15M / 2 yr, scaling up to $160M / 10 yr** for regional innovation ecosystems; the 2025–26 finalist cohort included **critical-minerals and energy-materials Engines**. Strong critical-minerals coalition fit (e.g., a Mountain West critical-minerals/advanced-manufacturing Engine with BYU + U of Utah + Ames/CMI partners), though BYU would join a coalition rather than lead. See [program details](#newly-identified-programs-june-2026) below.
- **NEW — MGI Autonomous Materials Innovation Infrastructure (AMII).** The 2024 cross-agency (NSF/DOE/NIST/DoD) MGI [AMII workshop report](https://www.mgi.gov/sites/mgi/files/MGI_Autonomous_Materials_Innovation_Infrastructure_Workshop_Report.pdf) signals coordinated interest in self-driving labs / autonomous experimentation for materials — FLAIME's exact paradigm. Watch for resulting DCLs and solicitations; an AMII-aligned framing strengthens both DMREF and Genesis Mission narratives.

### Scope Differentiation Strategy

Multiple simultaneous submissions require careful scope differentiation. NSF prohibits substantially similar proposals to different programs:

- **Genesis Mission Topic 3B**: Full FLAIME pipeline — closed-loop alloy discovery with critical-mineral-lean focus (Al-Zr, Al-Mn-Cr-Zr). DOE framing emphasizes national supply chain security.
- **NSF CAREER**: Foundational research methodology + education plan — autonomous materials discovery as a research paradigm. NSF framing emphasizes intellectual merit and broader impacts.
- **CDS&E**: Computational methodology focus — pKGFN/BOFN, CALPHAD integration, ML-guided screening. Distinct from experimental pipeline.
- **DMREF**: Multi-PI collaborative, MGI-aligned — integrated computation + experiment team effort. Broader scope than individual PI proposals.
- **FM**: Manufacturing systems perspective — autonomous manufacturing as a transformative paradigm. Workforce development component.

All active and pending proposals must be disclosed on Current & Pending / Other Support forms per NSF PAPPG and DOE policies.

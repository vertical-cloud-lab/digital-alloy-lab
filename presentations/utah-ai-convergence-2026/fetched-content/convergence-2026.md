> An AI Convergence Summit for Utah --- **June 23 / 24 2026**

## About the Convergence

Building on the success of last year's inaugural Utah AI Summit, the John and
Marcia Price College of Engineering will host **Utah AI Convergence**, a two-day
convening exploring how artificial intelligence is advancing research,
innovation, education, and society. The event will bring together faculty,
students, industry leaders, and innovators to forge new research and industry
partnerships.

The program will feature faculty research presentations, industry speakers, a
student poster competition, and workshops designed to encourage interdisciplinary
collaboration across the region.

Registration for the 2026 Utah AI Convergence is offered free of charge to all
attendees thanks to the event's sponsors.

- Location: 50 S Central Campus Dr, Salt Lake City, Utah 84112
  (John and Marcia Price College of Engineering, University of Utah)
- Registration: <https://admin.coe.utah.edu/fill-form/2026-ai-convergence>

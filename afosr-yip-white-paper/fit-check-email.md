Hello Dr. Sayir,

I plan to submit an FY27 YIP white paper (FA955026S0003) by October 9 and would like to check whether it fits Aerospace Materials for Extreme Environments. The BAA says proposals focused primarily on structural materials are not a fit, and my work uses refractory alloys.

The research question is how laser settings control the microstructure that forms when refractory alloys are mixed and solidified in the melt pool during laser powder bed fusion. I would build linked models of thermodynamics, melt tracks, and microstructure, with uncertainty quantification. They would predict how likely a composition and laser setting are to reach a target microstructure, and they would choose the next experiment. Today these alloys are developed largely by trial and error, one step at a time.

The plan is a single-PI effort at about $150K per year for three years. Does this fit your program, or would you suggest another portfolio?

## Where the wording comes from

All quotes are from BAA FA955026S0001, Amendment 0001 (2026-08-21), §A.3.a,
and the FY27 YIP NOFO FA955026S0003 (2026-09-08).

- The BAA asks researchers to contact the program manager first to *"briefly
  discuss the current state-of-the-art, how the proposed effort would advance
  it, and the approximate yearly cost."* The second paragraph covers the first
  two points and the third paragraph gives the cost.
- *"Proposals focused primarily on structural or organic materials … are not
  a fit for this program."* The email names this up front instead of leaving
  it for the PO to raise.
- The Materials Synthesis by Design subtopic asks how *"external stimuli—such
  as electric fields, lasers, or acoustic waves—can guide the formation of
  desired material structures"* and requires UQ that gives *"a predictive
  confidence level for achieving any target microstructure."* The email pitches
  laser-driven synthesis with quantified confidence. Ultrasonic atomization is
  left out because the induction module on hand can't melt refractory alloys.
- Checked with the cringe-filter `email` context (2026-10-08): no findings,
  169 words with the signature (budget 170). The sentence on mechanical tests
  was cut to get under the budget.

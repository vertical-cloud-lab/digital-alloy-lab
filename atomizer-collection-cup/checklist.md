# Run checklist: lab-built cup B (print me)

One page for the person at the atomizer. The reasons and numbers are in `procedure.md`. The upper part stays
on the cone; the pot comes off every run.

## Before the pot goes on
- [ ] Upper part on the cone: KF clamp closed finger-tight, centring ring and **over-pressure ring** in, top
      thermocouple clamped on the stub.
- [ ] **Shields in the cone's mouth:** at least one for aluminium; for copper alloys as many as AMAZEMET advises.
- [ ] Nothing inside the upper part: no splash plate, no valve, no insert.
- [ ] Pot clean and dry. Puck in, chamfer down; liner standing on it. Bottom thermocouple clamped on the cap's rim.
- [ ] Tri-clamp gasket: PTFE, fresh if the last run was a bad one.

## Putting the pot on
- [ ] Lift the pot under the upper part, fit the gasket, close the 3 in tri-clamp, plug in the bottom thermocouple.
- [ ] Safety lanyard from the pot to the frame, slack.
- [ ] Meter on. Alarms: **top 120 °C, bottom 150 °C**. Logger running if you have one.

## Before melting
- [ ] Rate of rise at full vacuum, pump valved off, 5 min. It should match the baseline: Edison's targets are about
      0.04 mbar/min (gross). A Nov-13-size leak climbs about 25–30 mbar/min.
- [ ] Sensor bleed flowing (blue cabinet). Above +50 mbar after a refill, O₂ falls below 1000 ppm within a minute.
- [ ] **O₂ at or below the SOP's limit (29 ppm) before the melt.** If not, find the leak; don't melt.

## During the pour
- [ ] Watch the plate. **No powder within 20–30 s** (shot, sparks, metal piling up): close the sealing rod.
- [ ] Top alarm: stop the pour. Metal is held near the KF seal.
- [ ] Bottom alarm about 2–3 min after the pour: it was a bad run. Let the pot cool.

## After
- [ ] Wait until the bottom thermocouple reads **below 60 °C**.
- [ ] Unplug the bottom thermocouple, unclip the lanyard, open the tri-clamp, lower the pot, fit the 3 in cap.
- [ ] Empty over a sieve or tray. The puck and liner come out with the powder; replace them if metal is welded on.
- [ ] **Top alarm went off:** replace the KF O-ring. **Bottom alarm went off:** replace the tri-clamp gasket.
      Note both peaks in the run record.

# Run checklist: lab-built cup B (print me)

One page for the person at the atomizer. The reasons and numbers are in `procedure.md`.

## Before the cup goes on
- [ ] Cup clean and dry inside. Puck sits flat on the floor, chamfer down; the liner drops in and stands on it.
- [ ] Centring ring (stainless, FKM O-ring) undamaged. **Over-pressure ring** to hand.
- [ ] Both thermocouples clamped (top on the stub under the flange, bottom just above the floor). Meter on.
      Alarms: **top 120 °C, bottom 150 °C**. Logger running if you have one.
- [ ] **Shields in the cone's mouth:** at least one for aluminium; for copper alloys as many as AMAZEMET advises.
- [ ] Nothing inside the top of the cup: no splash plate, no valve, no insert.

## Putting it on
- [ ] **Clamp OFF**, not hanging open: an open hinged clamp still blocks the flange.
- [ ] Two people: lift the cup straight up under the outlet, seat the ring, fit the over-pressure ring round the
      O-ring, close the clamp **finger-tight**.
- [ ] Safety lanyard from the cup to the frame, slack.
- [ ] Cup hangs straight; thermocouple leads clear of the door and the frame.

## Before melting
- [ ] Rate of rise at full vacuum, pump valved off, 5 min. Compare with the baseline; it should be well under
      1 mbar/min, and a Nov-13-size leak climbs about 25–30 mbar/min.
- [ ] Sensor bleed flowing (blue cabinet). Above +50 mbar after a refill, O₂ falls below 1000 ppm within a minute.
- [ ] **O₂ at or below the SOP's limit (29 ppm) before the melt.** If not, find the leak; don't melt.

## During the pour
- [ ] Watch the plate. **No powder within 20–30 s** (shot, sparks, metal piling up): close the sealing rod.
- [ ] Top alarm: stop the pour. Metal is held near the seal, so plan to replace the O-ring.
- [ ] Bottom alarm about 2 min after the pour: it was a bad run. Note it and let the cup cool before removal.

## After
- [ ] Wait until the bottom thermocouple reads **below 60 °C**.
- [ ] Clamp off, drop 5 mm, slide out toward you, KF blank and clamp on top.
- [ ] Empty over a sieve or tray. Puck and liner out; replace them if metal is welded on or they stick.
- [ ] Top thermocouple went over 150 °C: **replace the O-ring.** Note both peaks in the run record.
